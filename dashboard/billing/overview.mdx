### Plan Tiers

| Plan           | Price      |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| **Starter**    | $0         |
| **Growth**     | $199/month |
| **Pro**        | $799/month |
| **Enterprise** | Custom     |

Paid plans are billed monthly. See [Pricing](/dashboard/billing/pricing) for what each plan includes and its Fanfare checkout rates.

### Trial Period

A new organization that signs up for Growth or Pro, billed through Fanfare, starts with a 5-day trial of that plan. During the trial:

| Information        | Description                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Features**       | Everything in the plan you're trialling, at that plan's Fanfare checkout rates      |
| **Payment method** | Not required to start; add one before the trial ends to keep the plan               |
| **Plan changes**   | Need a payment method on file                                                       |
| **Trial ends**     | With a payment method, the plan continues and the first charge is made; without one, the subscription is cancelled and the organization moves to Starter |

Enterprise plans have no trial.

### Managing Payment via Stripe

Payment details are handled through Stripe's secure billing portal:

1. Click **Open billing portal** on the billing page
2. You're redirected to Stripe's billing portal
3. Update your card or billing details, or download invoices
4. Return to Fanfare when done

## Changing Plans

### Upgrading or Downgrading

To switch plans:

1. Go to the **Plans** section of the billing page
2. Click **Upgrade to** or **Switch to** on the plan you want
3. Choose when the change takes effect
4. Review the proration details and click **Confirm Change**

For Enterprise, click **Contact sales**.

### When Changes Apply

| Option                             | When Applied                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Change immediately**             | Now, with a prorated credit for unused time and a charge for the new plan |
| **Change at end of billing period** | At your next renewal                                              |

A new plan's features and Fanfare checkout rates apply from the moment the change takes effect. When you move to a lower plan, existing experiences, pages and integrations keep working; see [Pricing](/dashboard/billing/pricing) for what each plan lets you create and publish.

## Subscription Status

### Status Types

| Status        | Description                                          | Plan in effect     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Trial**     | Trial of a paid plan                                 | The trialled plan  |
| **Active**    | Subscription is current                              | Your plan          |
| **Past Due**  | A payment failed                                     | Your plan          |
| **Cancelled** | The subscription has ended                           | Starter            |
| **Pending**   | The subscription is still being set up               | Starter            |
| **Inactive**  | The subscription is inactive; contact support        | Starter            |

The plan in effect sets both the features you can use and your Fanfare checkout rates.

### Past Due Status

If your payment fails:

1. The billing page shows that your last payment didn't go through
2. Your plan stays in effect
3. Update your payment method to settle the invoice

### Cancelled Status

After a cancellation takes effect:

1. Your organization moves to Starter
2. Your settings and data stay in place
3. Reactivate on a paid plan at any time

## Invoices and Receipts

### Viewing Invoices

Access your invoice history:

1. Go to Billing
2. Click **Open billing portal**
3. View invoices in Stripe's billing portal
4. Download PDF invoices

### Invoice Contents

| Item               | Description           |
| ------------------ | --------------------- |
| **Invoice Number** | Unique identifier     |
| **Date**           | Invoice date          |
| **Amount**         | Total charged         |
| **Status**         | Paid, pending, failed |
| **Download**       | PDF download link     |

## Cancellation

### Cancelling Your Subscription

To cancel:

1. Go to Billing
2. Click **Cancel subscription**
3. Choose a reason and, optionally, share feedback
4. Choose when to cancel
5. Click **Confirm Cancellation**

### Cancellation Timing

| Option                        | What Happens                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cancel at end of period**   | Your plan stays in effect until the current period ends   |
| **Cancel immediately**        | Your plan ends now                                        |

Either way, no refund is made for the current period, and once the cancellation takes effect your organization moves to Starter.

## Reactivation

### Before the Period Ends

If you scheduled a cancellation and want to keep your plan, click **Keep subscription** on the billing page.

### After Cancellation

If your subscription has been cancelled:

1. Go to Billing
2. Click **Reactivate**, or choose a plan in the **Plans** section
3. Update your payment method if prompted
4. Confirm reactivation; the new plan is charged from that day

## Shopify Billing

If you installed the Fanfare app from the Shopify App Store:

| Feature          | Shopify Handling                              |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Billing**      | Your Fanfare plan is on your Shopify invoice  |
| **Plan changes** | From the Fanfare app in your Shopify admin    |
| **Invoices**     | Available in Shopify admin                    |
| **Cancellation** | In your Shopify admin                         |

<Info>
The billing page shows your Shopify-billed plan, but plan changes and payment details are managed in your Shopify admin, not in Fanfare.
</Info>

## Fanfare Checkout Fees

When consumers pay through Fanfare checkout:

| Fee             | Description                                                                |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fanfare fee** | A percentage of each paid order, set by your plan and the experience type |
| **Stripe fees** | Stripe's processing fees, charged directly to your Stripe account          |

Fanfare checkout fees apply only to payments taken through Fanfare checkout, not to orders consumers pay for through Shopify's own checkout. Fanfare checkout currently takes payments in US dollars, for organizations whose Stripe account is based in the United States.

See [Pricing](/dashboard/billing/pricing) for the rate on each plan, worked examples, and how refunds and disputes affect fees.

## Best Practices

### Billing Hygiene

1. **Keep payment current** - Update expired cards
2. **Download invoices** - Keep records
3. **Review charges** - Verify accuracy
4. **Set reminders** - For renewal dates

## Troubleshooting

### Payment Failed

1. Check card is valid
2. Ensure sufficient funds
3. Update payment method
4. Contact support if issues persist

### Can't Access Portal

1. Try refreshing the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try different browser
4. Contact support
