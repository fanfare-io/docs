---
title: Before doors open
description: What must be right before a drop opens, and what stops being changeable on its own as the opening time approaches.
---

Use this page in the days before a drop. There is no launch step: each distribution goes live at its **Opens** time with whatever is set at that moment, and until then the experience reads **Scheduled** (see [Experiences](/concepts/experiences)). The checklist below covers what has to be right well before the drop. The countdown after it shows what Fanfare changes on its own as **Opens** approaches, so you can finish each setting before it locks.

<Warning>
  Nothing asks you to launch the drop. At **Opens** customers start getting in on the settings as they stand. Some settings stop being changeable shortly before then. For payment collection, auction prices and a later opening time, the only way out is to deactivate the distribution and create a new one. Deactivating cannot be undone and shows your whole public page as ended, and once every distribution in a sequence is deactivated, the replacement needs a new sequence, or a new experience if nothing in the experience is left. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) and [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).
</Warning>

## Before you start

- The experience exists, with its sequences and distributions. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).
- You know who on your team is an organisation admin. Only admins can **Deactivate**, which is how you stop a drop. See [Team members](/guide/account/team).

## Well before the drop

Work through this list a day or more ahead. Each line points to the page that explains it.

1. Every distribution's **Opens** and **Closes** are the times you mean, not the wizard's defaults (**Opens At** the next minute, **Closes At** seven days later), and the experience's **Time Zone** is right. An experience created with the default opening goes live almost at once. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).
2. Distribution windows in one sequence do not overlap, and no distribution you rely on later is blocked by an earlier one with no close time. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
3. Your sequences were created in the order customers should be offered them. Priority follows creation order and cannot be changed in the admin. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
4. Who can enter each sequence is decided: its audience, its **Shared access code**, and **Allow guest access** on each distribution. Choosing an audience turns guest entry off, and an auction or appointment makes its sequence require sign-in. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences) and [Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences). Creating and editing audiences needs Growth or above.
5. Each audience you gate with reads **Live**, not **Not built yet**, **Building** or **Build failed**. An audience admits nobody until its first build finishes. See [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences).
6. Every size you sell has a number. A size left at 0 opens sold out, a size at **No limit** cannot be given a number once counting starts, and a sequence with more than 256 size combinations refuses new customers while it is counted. See [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation).
7. Payment is ready. If you sell through Fanfare checkout, Stripe is connected: see [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments). For each queue you have chosen **Pay when admitted** or **Pre-authorise on joining**, and for each draw **Pay after the win** or **Pre-authorise on entry**, because the choice locks shortly before opening. If you pre-authorise, keep entry close to selection: a card hold lasts about a week, so customers who enter long before winners are chosen can have their hold expire. See [Set up a queue](/guide/create/queue), [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw) and [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).
8. Distribution names read well to customers. The waitlist "opens soon" email uses each name as you typed it. See [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist).
9. Customers have somewhere to go: the hosted page is published, or the widget is on your own site. Publish your final page before the freeze in the countdown below. See [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) and [Run it on your own site](/guide/create/your-own-site). Hosted pages need Growth or above.
10. Announcements are scheduled and test-sent, and the invite email is set for each sequence gated by an audience. See [Announcements](/guide/create/announcements) and [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist). Your sender identity, including your postal address, is set up: see [Sender identity and sending domain](/guide/create/email-sender).
11. Guest access is on only where customers will keep the page open. Guests get no emails or texts. See [What your customers see](/guide/drop/what-customers-see).
12. If a queue or draw uses **Bias toward loyal consumers**, your loyalty programme is switched on. Loyalty needs Pro or above. See [Run a loyalty programme](/guide/learn/loyalty) and [Loyalty and Fanfair](/concepts/loyalty).
13. Your plan covers every feature the drop uses. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).
14. Your brand and widget designs are final. Saving them restyles live pages without a publish. See [Set your brand](/guide/create/brand) and [Templates and widget designs](/guide/create/templates).

## As opening approaches

These things happen on their own, counted back from a distribution's **Opens** time. A queue starts earlier than the other types because its waiting room opens 5 minutes before **Opens**.

  <img src="/images/guide/guide-drop-preflight-countdown.webp" alt="A countdown timeline that ends at a drop's Opens time. The invite email sends 2 days before by default. At 15 minutes, republishing the hosted page freezes. At about 10 minutes, stock counting starts, sizes can only go up and products can lock, and a queue's gating audience rebuilds. At about 5 minutes 30 seconds, a queue's payment settings lock. At 5 minutes, the waiting room opens and the other types' audiences rebuild. At 30 seconds, the settings of draws, auctions and timed releases lock. At Opens, customers start getting in and the experience reads Live." />
</Frame>

| Time before **Opens** | What happens | What it means for you | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 days, by default, before the sequence's first **Opens** | The invite email sends to the sequence's audience. You can change the timing under **When it sends** until it first sends. If the sequence opens sooner than that, it sends about 30 minutes after the invite is set up. | The audience, the published hosted page and your postal address need to be in place before then. | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| 15 minutes (counted from any distribution in the experience) | Republishing a hosted page that is already published is refused until the distribution closes, or until 30 minutes after it opens if it has no close time, unless an admin confirms **Override freeze and publish**. A first publish is never frozen. | Publish your final page before this point. | [Brand, templates and designs](/concepts/design) |
| About 10 minutes | Stock counting starts on a sequence with counted stock. From then a size can only go up, the sequence's product selection can lock, and the experience's products cannot be added or removed. For a queue, a **Dynamic** audience that gates the sequence rebuilds now. | Set every size and the products on offer before this point. Get audience rules right before the rebuild. | [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when), [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation), [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| About 5 minutes 30 seconds (queue) | The queue counts as opened. Payment collection and **Authorization amount** lock, and **Opens** can only move earlier. | Check the queue's payment settings before this point. | [Set up a queue](/guide/create/queue) |
| 5 minutes | A queue's waiting room opens and customers can join it. The sequence's waitlist "opens soon" message sends (appointments have no waitlist). For the other types, a **Dynamic** audience that gates the sequence rebuilds now. | Customers start arriving at a queue from here. | [Queue](/concepts/distributions/queue), [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist), [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| 30 seconds (draw, auction, timed release) | The distribution counts as opened. **Opens** can only move earlier. A draw's payment collection, **Authorization amount** and **Continue selecting until completed** lock. An auction's prices and quantity are fixed. | Check these settings before this point. | [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw), [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) |
| At **Opens** | Customers start getting in, and the distribution, its sequence and the experience read **Live**. | Watch the drop. | [Watch a live drop](/guide/drop/watch-live) |

The audience hold has no fixed time. Once a gated distribution starts taking entries (for a queue, when its waiting room opens 5 minutes before **Opens**), full rebuilds of a **Dynamic** audience that gates it wait, and **Refresh now** is refused. No hold applies while any distribution taking entries has no end: a queue, timed release or appointment with no **Closes**. A draw with no **Closes** still holds until its draw time. See [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences).

An appointment's window, slot length, buffer, capacity and location cannot change while any booking stands, and customers can book as soon as the page is live, which can be well before **Opens**. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when).

## Check the forecast

On the sequence's **Performance** tab, **Before opening** shows **Opens in**, **Expected entrants** and **Range**. You see "No forecast yet" until this experience has at least two closed distributions of the same kind (English and Dutch auctions count separately). What the figures mean is in [Read your results](/guide/learn/performance#before-a-distribution-opens).

## Have the controls ready

- Some settings stay editable once the drop is live, such as a queue's **Admit rate** and **Closes**, and a save takes effect with no confirmation step. See [Change things mid-drop](/guide/drop/live-controls).
- Keep the live figures open while the drop runs. See [Watch a live drop](/guide/drop/watch-live).
- To stop a drop, an admin selects **Deactivate**, which cannot be undone. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).
- When something goes wrong mid-drop, start at [Troubleshooting a live drop](/guide/drop/troubleshooting).

## On Shopify

In the Fanfare app in Shopify, check these as well:

- Both storefront steps are done: checkout protection is on and the product widget is on your theme. **Home** shows "Fanfare is set up on your store" when both are; until then it says "Your experiences won't protect a product on your store until both steps are done." See [Storefront setup](/guide/shopify/storefront-setup).
- The products in the drop came in from Shopify and are up to date. Select **Sync from Shopify** on a product to bring a change in at once. See [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products).
- There is no payment collection choice to make in item 7: customers pay in Shopify's checkout. See [Checkout options](/concepts/checkout-options).
- An auction needs your store on Shopify Plus, Fanfare's latest permissions approved in Shopify, checkout protection on, and your store's currency the same as your organisation's. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
- Appointments are not offered.
- A queue or draw size that Shopify manages starts at Shopify's count when you create it. See [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation#on-shopify).
- Every Shopify staff member who can open the app counts as an organisation admin, so anyone there can deactivate. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).
