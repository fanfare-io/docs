title: Before doors open
description: Final pre-launch checklist and automatic lock countdown before your drop opens.
---

Run through this checklist a day or two before launch. Fanfare has no separate publish or launch button—each distribution opens automatically at its **Opens** time with whatever settings are saved at that moment, moving from **Scheduled** to **Live** (see [Experiences](/concepts/experiences)).

The checklist below covers what to lock in ahead of time, followed by a countdown of what Fanfare locks automatically as doors open.

<Warning>
  Drops go live automatically at **Opens**, and several settings lock shortly beforehand. Once locked, you can't change payment collection, auction prices, or a delayed opening time without deactivating the distribution and creating a new one. **Deactivate** can't be undone, marks your entire public page as ended, and if every distribution in a sequence or experience is deactivated, you'll need a new sequence or experience to relaunch. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) and [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).
</Warning>

## Before you start

- Your experience, sequences, and distributions are built. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).
- You know who on your team is an organization admin—only admins can **Deactivate** a drop if you need to stop it. See [Team members](/guide/account/team).

## Pre-launch checklist

Review each item at least a day before your drop:

1. **Schedule and time zone:** Confirm every distribution's **Opens** and **Closes** times and the experience's **Time Zone**. Make sure you replaced the wizard defaults (**Opens At** defaults to the next minute, **Closes At** to 7 days later) so the drop doesn't open early. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).
2. **Non-overlapping windows:** Verify that distribution windows inside the same sequence don't overlap, and that no earlier distribution without a close time blocks a later one. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
3. **Sequence priority:** Confirm your sequences were created in the order you want customers offered them—priority follows creation order and can't be reordered in the admin. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
4. **Access and gating:** Check each sequence's audience, **Shared access code**, and per-distribution **Allow guest access** setting. Adding an audience disables guest entry, and auctions or appointments require sign-in for the entire sequence. Creating and editing audiences requires Growth or above. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences) and [Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences).
5. **Audience status:** Make sure every gating audience shows **Live**—not **Not built yet**, **Building**, or **Build failed**. An audience won't admit anyone until its first build finishes. See [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences).
6. **Stock counts:** Confirm every size you plan to sell has a count. A size left at `0` opens sold out, a size set to **No limit** can't switch to a fixed count once stock counting starts, and any sequence with more than 256 size combinations refuses new customers while counted. See [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation).
7. **Payment settings:** If you use Fanfare checkout, confirm Stripe is connected ([Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments)). Lock in **Pay when admitted** or **Pre-authorize on joining** for each queue, and **Pay after the win** or **Pre-authorize on entry** for each draw—these lock shortly before opening. When pre-authorizing, keep entry close to selection: card holds expire after about 7 days, so early entrants can lose their hold before winners are chosen. See [Set up a queue](/guide/create/queue), [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw), and [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).
8. **Customer-facing distribution names:** Give every distribution a clean name—waitlist "opens soon" emails display distribution names verbatim. See [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist).
9. **Published destination:** Publish your hosted page (Growth and above) or confirm the widget is live on your site. Finish hosted page edits before the 15-minute publish freeze below. See [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) and [Run it on your own site](/guide/create/your-own-site).
10. **Announcements, invites, and sender identity:** Schedule and test-send announcements, review invite emails for audience-gated sequences, and confirm your sender identity and postal address are saved. See [Announcements](/guide/create/announcements), [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist), and [Sender identity and sending domain](/guide/create/email-sender).
11. **Guest access check:** Only turn on **Allow guest access** where customers will keep the page open—guests don't receive emails or texts. See [What your customers see](/guide/drop/what-customers-see).
12. **Loyalty program (if used):** If any queue or draw uses **Bias toward loyal consumers**, confirm your loyalty program (Pro and above) is turned on. See [Run a loyalty program](/guide/learn/loyalty) and [Loyalty and Fanfair](/concepts/loyalty).
13. **Plan coverage:** Make sure your current plan covers every feature in the drop. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).
14. **Brand and widget designs:** Finalize colors, fonts, and widget styling now. Saving brand changes restyles live pages immediately without republishing. See [Set your brand](/guide/create/brand) and [Templates and widget designs](/guide/create/templates).

## Countdown to opening

As a distribution's **Opens** time approaches, Fanfare runs a set of automatic steps and locks. Queues begin earlier because their waiting room opens 5 minutes before **Opens**.

<Frame caption="Automatic locks and events as a distribution's Opens time approaches.">
  <img src="/images/guide/guide-drop-preflight-countdown.webp" alt="A countdown timeline ending at a drop's Opens time. The invite email sends 2 days before by default. At 15 minutes, republishing the hosted page freezes. At about 10 minutes, stock counting starts, sizes can only increase, products can lock, and a queue's gating audience rebuilds. At about 5 minutes 30 seconds, a queue's payment settings lock. At 5 minutes, the waiting room opens and other types' audiences rebuild. At 30 seconds, draw, auction, and timed release settings lock. At Opens, customers enter and the experience reads Live." />
</Frame>

| Time before **Opens** | What happens automatically | What to finish beforehand | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 days (default) before the sequence's first **Opens** | Fanfare sends the invite email to the sequence's audience. (You can adjust **When it sends** until the first send; if the sequence opens sooner, it sends about 30 minutes after setup.) | Finalize your audience, publish your hosted page, and save your postal address. | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| 15 minutes (any distribution in the experience) | Republishing an already-published hosted page freezes until the distribution closes—or 30 minutes after opening if it has no close time—unless an admin selects **Override freeze and publish**. First-time publishes are never frozen. | Publish your final hosted page. | [Brand, templates and designs](/concepts/design) |
| About 10 minutes | Stock counting starts on counted sequences. From here, size counts can only increase, sequence products can lock, and experience products can't be added or removed. For queues, gating **Dynamic** audiences rebuild now. | Finalize stock allocations, product selection, and queue audience rules. | [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when), [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation), [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| About 5 minutes 30 seconds (queue) | The queue counts as opened. Payment collection and **Authorization amount** lock, and **Opens** can only move earlier. | Double-check queue payment settings. | [Set up a queue](/guide/create/queue) |
| 5 minutes | A queue's waiting room opens and customers can join the line. Waitlist "opens soon" messages send (appointments don't have waitlists). For draws, auctions, timed releases, and appointments, gating **Dynamic** audiences rebuild now. | Expect queue traffic to start arriving. | [Queue](/concepts/distributions/queue), [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist), [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| 30 seconds (draw, auction, timed release) | The distribution counts as opened. **Opens** can only move earlier. Draw payment collection, **Authorization amount**, and **Continue selecting until completed** lock; auction prices and quantity lock. | Confirm draw payment and auction pricing settings. | [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw), [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) |
| At **Opens** | Customers start entering, and the distribution, sequence, and experience show **Live**. | Switch to live monitoring. | [Watch a live drop](/guide/drop/watch-live) |

<Note>
  **Dynamic audience holds:** Once a gated distribution starts taking entries (5 minutes before **Opens** for a queue), full rebuilds of any gating **Dynamic** audience pause and **Refresh now** is disabled until the drop closes. No hold applies while an open queue, timed release, or appointment has no **Closes** time; an open-ended draw still holds until its draw time. See [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences).
</Note>

<Note>
  **Appointments lock on first booking:** An appointment's window, slot duration, buffer, capacity, and location lock as soon as any booking exists—and customers can book as soon as the page is live, even well before **Opens**. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when).
</Note>

## Check the demand forecast

Open your sequence's **Performance** tab to check **Before opening**, which shows **Opens in**, **Expected entrants**, and **Range**. Forecasts appear once the experience has at least two closed distributions of the same type (English and Dutch auctions count separately); until then, you'll see "No forecast yet." See [Read your results](/guide/learn/performance#before-a-distribution-opens).

## Keep live controls handy

- **Live edits apply immediately:** Settings that stay editable mid-drop—such as a queue's **Admit rate** and **Closes**—take effect as soon as you save, with no confirmation dialog. See [Change things mid-drop](/guide/drop/live-controls).
- **Keep live monitoring open:** Follow queue length, admissions, and sales in real time in [Watch a live drop](/guide/drop/watch-live).
- **Know how to stop a drop:** Only organization admins can **Deactivate**, and it can't be undone. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).
- **Bookmark troubleshooting:** If anything looks off during the drop, start at [Troubleshooting a live drop](/guide/drop/troubleshooting).

## On Shopify

If you run drops through the Fanfare app in Shopify, check these items too:

- **Storefront setup is complete:** Both checkout protection and the product widget must be active. **Home** shows "Fanfare is set up on your store" when ready; otherwise it warns that your experiences won't protect products until both steps are done. See [Storefront setup](/guide/shopify/storefront-setup).
- **Products are synced:** Your drop's products come from Shopify—select **Sync from Shopify** on any product to pull in recent catalog changes immediately. See [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products).
- **Checkout is handled by Shopify:** You can skip payment collection settings in item 7—customers pay in Shopify's checkout. See [Checkout options](/concepts/checkout-options).
- **Auction requirements:** Running an auction requires Shopify Plus, Fanfare's latest approved app permissions, checkout protection enabled, and a store currency matching your Fanfare organization's currency. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
- **Shopify-managed stock:** Queue and draw sizes managed by Shopify start at Shopify's current inventory count when you create them. See [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation#on-shopify).
- **No appointments:** Appointments aren't available in the Shopify app.
- **Admin permissions:** Every Shopify staff member who can open the Fanfare app counts as an organization admin and can **Deactivate** a drop. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).
