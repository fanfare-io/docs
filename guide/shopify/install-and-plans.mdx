---
title: Install and choose a plan
description: Install the Fanfare app on your Shopify store, approve a plan on Shopify's plan page, and fix what stops you getting into the app.
---

You install Fanfare from Shopify, and you pay for it through Shopify. There is no separate Fanfare sign-up or sign-in: installing the app creates your Fanfare organisation, and every store then approves a plan on Shopify's own plan page before it can use the app. This page covers the install, choosing and changing a plan, how Fanfare's fees reach your Shopify bill, and what to do when the app will not let you in.

## Before you start

- You can install apps on the store and approve the permissions Shopify asks for.
- Your store's currency is one Fanfare can price.

Fanfare can price every currency Shopify offers except USD Coin (USDC), the Venezuelan digital bolívar (VED), the Kiribati dollar (KID), and the withdrawn or placeholder codes VEF, BYR, LTL, LVL and XXX.

<Warning>
  If Fanfare cannot price your store's currency, the whole app shows only one message: "Fanfare isn't available for stores priced in … yet. Change your store's currency to use Fanfare.", with your currency code in place of the dots. No other page is reachable, not even Billing. Change the store's currency in Shopify and open the app again; the check runs every time a page loads.
</Warning>

{/* image: install-and-plans-currency-notice */}

## Install the app

1. Install the Fanfare app from Shopify.
2. Approve the permissions Shopify shows.
3. Shopify opens the Fanfare app. Fanfare creates your organisation automatically, in your store's currency. There is no step to connect an existing Fanfare organisation.

The app opens on Shopify's plan page next, because your store has no plan yet.

## Choose a plan the first time

Until Shopify confirms a plan for your store, opening any part of the Fanfare app sends you to Shopify's plan page, or to **Confirming your plan** if you have just approved one. Only the Billing pages, including **Usage**, stay reachable in the meantime.

<Warning>
  Every store must approve a plan on Shopify's plan page before it can use Fanfare, Starter included, unless it is comped or still on an older Fanfare subscription (see below). Until you do, you cannot reach Home, Experiences or any other page.
</Warning>

1. On Shopify's plan page, pick a plan: Starter, Growth or Pro. Enterprise appears there only for a store Fanfare has set it up for.
2. Approve it in Shopify.
3. Shopify sends you back to Fanfare. If Fanfare can confirm the plan straight away, the app opens; otherwise you see a page titled **Confirming your plan** until it does.

{/* image: install-and-plans-plan-page */}

What each plan unlocks, and what happens to things you already built when you move to a lower plan, is on [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).

### While Fanfare confirms your plan

Shopify can take a moment to tell Fanfare about a plan you have just approved. The **Confirming your plan** page says "Fanfare is confirming the plan you approved. This usually takes a few seconds, and can take a few minutes." The page rechecks on its own and opens the app as soon as the plan is confirmed. You do not need to reload it.

If the plan is still not confirmed after about seven minutes, the page stops checking and says "Fanfare is still confirming your plan, which is taking longer than usual. Please contact Fanfare support so we can finish setting it up." Where you reached the page from Fanfare rather than straight from Shopify, it also shows **Try again**, which takes you back into the app.

If Fanfare cannot tell which plan you approved, the page says so and shows **Continue to Fanfare**, which also takes you back into the app. If either button brings you back to this page, wait a few minutes before trying again, or contact Fanfare support.

{/* image: install-and-plans-confirming */}

### After the app opens

The app starts a short setup tour. Finish both storefront steps before you run a drop: checkout protection, which Fanfare usually turns on for you at install, and the product widget, which you add to your theme yourself. Once a product is in an experience, checkout protection refuses ordinary checkouts of it even if the widget is not on your theme, and without the widget customers have no way to take part from the product page. Check both on **Settings → Storefront setup**; see [Storefront setup](/guide/shopify/storefront-setup). For a first drop from start to finish, follow [Your first drop on Shopify](/guide/start/your-first-drop-on-shopify).

## Change your plan later

1. In Shopify admin, open the Fanfare app's menu and select **Billing**.
2. Fanfare opens Shopify's plan page in place of the Fanfare app. If nothing happens, select **Open it here if it didn't open.**
3. Pick and approve the new plan in Shopify. Shopify sends you back to Fanfare, as on first install: the app opens, or **Confirming your plan** shows until Fanfare confirms the plan.

{/* image: install-and-plans-nav */}

The Fanfare app in Shopify has no plan cards, prices, invoices, payment method or cancel screens. All of that is on Shopify's side. If someone in your organisation also uses the Fanfare admin outside Shopify, its billing page shows "Billed through Shopify", and every plan button there reads **Manage in Shopify** and is disabled; see [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing).

If your plan is cancelled or stops being active, opening the app sends you back to Shopify's plan page, and only the Billing pages stay reachable until you approve a plan again, Starter included. What happens to experiences you already built is on [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).

### Stores on an older Fanfare subscription

If your store subscribed to Fanfare before the current plans, the app lets you in on that subscription and shows a banner. It gives the date your existing subscription stays active until, in UTC, and says "The Fanfare plan you choose in Shopify starts when it ends." When Shopify gives no end date, the banner says the subscription stays active "until its current billing period ends". Any plan you choose in Shopify starts only when the old subscription ends.

### Comped stores

A store that Fanfare provides at no charge sees "Your plan is comped: Fanfare provides it at no charge, so there is no plan to choose or pay for here." when it opens **Billing**, and is never sent to Shopify's plan page.

## Fanfare's fees on Shopify checkout orders

When a customer buys through a Fanfare experience and pays at Shopify's checkout, Fanfare's fee on that order is normally charged on your Shopify bill, in US dollars. Fees are calculated on your sales in your store currency and converted to USD at the daily reference rate for the date the order was paid, in UTC. **Usage** in the Fanfare menu shows the fees recorded in each Shopify billing cycle, where each one is charged, and any fee that was not billed and why. How to read that page is on [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing), and the rates are on [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments).

## Approve new permissions

Fanfare sometimes needs a permission your store has not granted.

- If a permission Fanfare's store connection needs is missing, opening the app sends you to approve it. Until you do, syncing a product, importing an order and pushing an order to Shopify ask you to reconnect the store.
- Two permissions work differently: reading your store's themes, and reading your store's locations, which Fanfare uses to count inventory. Fanfare asks for both at install, with the rest. If either is missing, Fanfare does not send you to approve it and the rest of the app keeps working, but **Settings → Storefront setup** shows the product widget step as **Couldn't check** (themes), or inventory counts for products from Shopify stop updating (locations).

Which staff in your store can open the Fanfare app, and what they can do in it, is on [Fanfare in Shopify](/guide/shopify/overview).

## Uninstall the app

You uninstall Fanfare in Shopify, like any other app. When you do:

- Your Fanfare organisation moves to Starter, cancelled.
- Fees in the Shopify billing cycle that is open are billed when that cycle ends, unless the billing window closes first. Fees after that are not billed. **Usage** marks any fee that was not billed.

If you install Fanfare again, you approve a plan on Shopify's plan page again before you can use the app.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The app says Fanfare isn't available for my store's currency">
    Fanfare cannot price your store's currency, so the whole app is replaced by this message and nothing else is reachable. Change your store's currency in Shopify, then open the Fanfare app again. The check runs on every page load, so the app opens as soon as the currency is one Fanfare supports.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm stuck on 'Confirming your plan'">
    Wait on the page: it rechecks by itself for about seven minutes and opens the app once Shopify confirms the plan. If it then asks you to contact Fanfare support, do so. If it shows **Try again** or **Continue to Fanfare**, select it to go back into the app. If you land on the same page again, wait a few minutes before trying again, or contact Fanfare support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shopify's plan page did not open">
    Selecting **Billing** opens Shopify's plan page in place of the Fanfare app. If your browser blocked it, select **Open it here if it didn't open.** on the page that stays behind.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm asked to approve permissions again">
    Fanfare needs a permission your store has not granted yet, and its store connection depends on it. Approve it to get back into the app. What stops working while a permission is missing is under [Approve new permissions](#approve-new-permissions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
