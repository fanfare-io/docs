title: Set up the next drop from this one
description: Turn last drop's results into your next release—decide whether to reuse your experience, build targeted audiences, bring back your waitlist, and reward loyal buyers.
---

Every finished drop gives you signal for the next one: how fast sizes moved, where shoppers dropped off, and who waited in line without getting through. Wait until your previous distributions read **Closed** on the **Performance** tab before making decisions—while a drop is **Settling**, admitted customers can still pay and numbers can change (refunds issued later still reduce **Net revenue**). See [Read your results](/guide/learn/performance).

<Note>
  Custom audiences require **Growth** or above; loyalty tiers and the loyalty program require **Pro** or above. Customers only earn loyalty points while the program is turned on, so drops that ran with Loyalty off won't have accrued points (see [Run a loyalty program](/guide/learn/loyalty)).
</Note>

## Review what happened

Four sections on the **Performance** tab tell you what to adjust next time:

- **By drop** (on the experience's **Performance** tab) — Compare entrants, paid orders, revenue, and sell-through across sequences side by side.
- **Entry to payment** — Check conversion from entry to admission to completed payment.
- **How queue places ended** (queues only) — High **Still waiting at close** means demand outpaced your window or stock; high **Admission lapsed** signals checkout friction or a tight payment window (see [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment)).
- **Sizes** — See which sizes sold out first and which lagged so you can rebalance stock next time.

## Decide where the next drop goes

<Warning>
  For products with **Fanfare-managed stock**, inventory is released automatically once every distribution in a sequence finishes and no one remains in checkout. A **Released** sequence can't accept new queues, draws, auctions, or timed releases—and once every stocked sequence in an experience is released, you can't allocate more stock to that experience. Create a new experience to sell those products again. Stock release can't be undone; see [Stock](/concepts/stock).
</Warning>

When stock allows you to stay in the same experience, keeping it preserves your demand history: **Expected entrants** forecasts and prior-drop comparisons only pull from closed distributions of the same type in the same experience (after one closed draw, the next draw compares against it; after two, it also gets a forecast). Starting a new experience resets both. See [Before a distribution opens](/guide/learn/performance#before-a-distribution-opens).

| Option | Best for | What you keep | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Same experience, new distribution in an existing sequence** | Running another window for the same audience with the same rules | Audience, access code, order limits, waitlist, forecast, and comparisons | Unavailable once the sequence is **Released** or if every distribution in it was deactivated. Use **Duplicate** on the distribution's menu to copy settings with dates cleared ([Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences)). Windows can't overlap, and a distribution with no close time blocks later ones. Waitlists reopen between windows ([Waitlists](/concepts/waitlists)). |
| **Same experience, new sequence** | Letting a new group in on different terms or from its own stock | Forecast, comparisons, products, and hosted page | Unavailable for Fanfare-managed products once experience stock is released. New sequences rank after earlier ones—priority follows creation order and can't be reordered ([Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences)). |
| **New experience** | New products, fully deactivated experiences, or released Fanfare-managed stock | Organization-wide audiences, tiers, and customer profiles | Required if the old experience was fully deactivated ([Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate)) or its Fanfare-managed stock was released. Starts without forecasts or comparisons, and needs its own hosted page ([Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page)). |

There's no way to copy an experience—create a new one and set it up again (see [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences)).

## Choose who gets in first

<Info>
  Custom rule audiences and CSV imports require **Growth** or above. Built-in **RFM Segments** (**Champions**, **Loyal**, **At-Risk**, **New**, **Lost**) can gate a sequence on every plan, including Starter. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).
</Info>

Gate an early-access sequence with an audience built from purchase or loyalty signals—such as **Total orders**, **Last order**, **Lifetime value**, **Average order value**, **Loyalty balance**, **Lifetime points**, engagement scores, **Created at**, or **Email** (full list in [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences)).

Because audience rules don't filter by a specific past drop, use purchase rules below—or export that drop's waitlist in the next section—to reach the right shoppers.

### Audience recipes

Create these from **Audiences → Create Audience**:

- **Repeat buyers:** **Match consumers where** **AND**, with **Total orders** **Greater Than or Equal** `2` and **Last order** **Greater Than** a date just before your last drop opened.
- **Top spenders:** **Lifetime value** **Greater Than or Equal** your target threshold.

{/* image: guide-learn-next-drop-repeat-buyers */}

Keep three operational details in mind before opening a gated sequence:

- **Fixed date rules:** **Last order** stays set to the exact date you entered until you update it—it doesn't roll forward automatically.
- **Experience-wide order limits:** Order limits count a customer's earlier purchases across the entire experience within the limit's time window. If you're letting repeat buyers back into the same experience, review **Edit → Order limits** on the sequence so they aren't blocked ([Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences)).
- **Wait for Live status:** Build your audience ahead of launch and verify it reads **Live** before opening—an audience admits no one until its first build finishes ([Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences)).

## Bring back last drop's waitlist

<Info>
  Exporting a waitlist works on every plan; importing it as a Static List audience requires **Growth** or above.
</Info>

A sequence's waitlist holds signed-in shoppers who asked to be notified when it opens (plus anyone you added manually)—it isn't everyone who entered. Because Fanfare doesn't export entrant, winner, or buyer lists (and **Customers** has no export), exporting the waitlist and importing it as a Static List audience is the cleanest way to give waiting shoppers first access next time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export the waitlist">
    Open the previous sequence, go to the **Waitlist** tab, and select **Exports → New export**. You can leave the page while it runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and combine files">
    Once the export reads **Completed** under **Past exports**, download every file. Exports split at 1,000 rows per file—if you have multiple files, merge them into one CSV and delete the repeated header rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter to people still waiting">
    Keep rows where `status` is `waitlisted` or `notified`, and remove `converted`, `expired`, and `left` rows (see [Waitlists](/concepts/waitlists)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Strip extra columns">
    Delete every column except `email` (keep `phone` too only if you need to include phone-only shoppers, and import right away).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import as a Static List">
    Go to **Audiences → Create Audience → Import CSV**, name the audience, upload your cleaned file, and select **Start Import**. See [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never import the raw waitlist export without removing extra columns. The export snapshots each customer's name, phone number, and lifetime points at export time. Importing those columns overwrites existing profiles and can't be undone; a phone number that changed since export is written back, which clears SMS consent. Keep only `email` (and `phone` if required).
</Warning>

{/* image: guide-learn-next-drop-waitlist-export */}

A **Static List** audience is created before the file uploads and can't have more files appended later—import your complete, cleaned list in one upload. If an upload fails, delete the empty audience and try again. New customers created from a CSV import start opted out of SMS marketing.

## Reward loyalty

<Info>Available on **Pro** and above.</Info>

- **Gate by loyalty tier:** Select one tier directly on your members' sequence. To admit multiple tiers together, build a rule-based audience instead; for tiers built on **Lifetime points**, use **Lifetime points** **Greater Than or Equal** the lowest tier threshold, or **Between** two thresholds. A plain rule audience like this only requires **Growth**. See [Loyalty and Fanfair](/concepts/loyalty).
- **Bias toward loyal consumers:** Turn this toggle on in your next queue or draw to give higher-point customers better odds (requires the loyalty program to be active; improves odds without guaranteeing a win).
- **Tune Event Points ahead of launch:** Raise **Purchase**, **Queue Events**, or **Draw Events** under **Loyalty → Event Points** before opening if you want the next drop to reward more points (browsing and cart cards don't award points, and slider changes apply within a few minutes to future awards only). See [Run a loyalty program](/guide/learn/loyalty).

## Put it together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create sequences in priority order">
    Create your early-access (members') sequence before your public sequence—routing priority follows creation order and can't be reordered later ([Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gate and schedule the early window">
    **Attaching an audience schedules an invite email—read Step 3 before you save.** Attach your audience or loyalty tier to the members' sequence and give it the earlier opening window. An open sequence is always offered ahead of a scheduled one, regardless of priority ([Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the automatic invite email right away">
    As soon as a sequence has an audience and a scheduled distribution, Fanfare queues an invite email for 2 days before opening (or about 30 minutes after saving if it opens sooner), provided your hosted page is published and your postal address is saved. Check the **Invite email** card on the sequence's **Access** tab right away and toggle off **Send this invite** if you aren't ready. Each customer is invited once per sequence, so reusing an existing sequence won't re-invite earlier invitees ([Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your preflight check">
    Walk through [Before doors open](/guide/drop/preflight) before launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example: running a follow-up drop

Northbound Supply Co. just closed **"Stealth Parka Winter Draw"** (one sequence, **"Public Draw"**, with a closed draw named **"Winter Draw"**):

1. **Reuse the experience:** The parka's stock is **Not managed** by Fanfare, so nothing was released and Northbound stays in the same experience to keep its demand history.
2. **Build a repeat-buyer audience:** They create **"Winter Draw Repeat Buyers"** (**Total orders** **Greater Than or Equal** `2` **AND** **Last order** **Greater Than** a date just before Winter Draw opened) and confirm it reads **Live**.
3. **Add an early-access sequence:** Because **"Public Draw"** was created first and holds higher priority, Northbound schedules the new members' draw to open and close before the next public draw opens.
4. **Build up forecasts:** The members' draw compares its results against Winter Draw (showing **"No forecast yet"** until two draws have closed), and the following public draw receives an **Expected entrants** forecast based on both closed draws.
5. **Re-engage the waitlist:** They export the Public Draw waitlist, keep `waitlisted` and `notified` rows with only the `email` column, and import it as a new Static List audience.
6. **Launch a new product separately:** For an upcoming sneaker, Northbound creates a new experience (**"KaiZen Relic Release"**), gates the initial sequence with **"Sneaker Club"**, adds a public sequence after it, and publishes its hosted page before invites send.

## On Shopify

Audiences, loyalty, waitlists, and **Performance** tabs work the same in the Fanfare Shopify app. Keep in mind that Shopify customer lists can't be imported directly as Fanfare audiences (see [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences)), products come from your Shopify catalog (see [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products)), and appointments aren't available in the Shopify app.
