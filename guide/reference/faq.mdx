## Setting up a drop

### How do I publish or launch my experience?

There's no separate launch step. Selecting **Create experience** schedules your drop right away—it becomes **Scheduled** as soon as a distribution has a future opening time, and switches to **Live** the moment that distribution opens. See [Experiences](/concepts/experiences) and [Status reference](/guide/reference/statuses).

### Why did my drop open as soon as I created it?

In the experience wizard, **Opens At** defaults to the next minute, and there's no separate publish button holding it back. If you select **Create experience** without changing the start time, the drop opens almost immediately. Set your desired opening time before finishing the wizard. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).

### How do I make one sequence come before another?

A sequence is one entry path into an experience. Sequence priority follows creation order (lowest number first) and can't be changed in the admin, so create your earliest or most exclusive sequence first. Because an open sequence always takes priority over a scheduled one regardless of number, give it the earlier opening window too. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).

### Can one sequence run a queue and then a draw?

Yes. A single sequence can run any mix of distributions back to back—their windows just can't overlap. If you want two distributions running at the same time, put them in separate sequences. See [Distributions](/concepts/distributions/overview).

### Do I need to add a waitlist, and does it catch people when my queue is full?

Every sequence (except appointment-only sequences) includes a built-in waitlist—there's nothing to set up. It accepts sign-ups from signed-in customers only when nothing in the sequence is live and another distribution is upcoming (or nothing is scheduled yet), so it doesn't act as an overflow list for a busy live queue. See [Waitlists](/concepts/waitlists).

### Are access codes case-sensitive?

No. Customers can enter an access code in uppercase, lowercase, or any mix. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).

### Why can't members of my audience get in?

An audience doesn't admit anyone until its first build finishes, so verify its status reads **Live** before the sequence opens. Audience members also have to sign in—sequences gated by an audience always require sign-in. See [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences).

### Can customers enter without an account?

Only in queues, draws, and timed releases with **Allow guest access** turned on—and only when every live or scheduled distribution in that sequence allows guests. Audience-gated sequences never allow guests. Keep in mind that guests don't receive emails or texts, so someone who closes the page can miss their turn without getting notified. See [Customers and identity](/concepts/customers).

### Can I switch a product to Fanfare-managed later?

No. **Stock management** locks when you create the product. If you need a different mode, create a new product with the right setting. If your organization uses an external checkout, every product you create is automatically **Not managed**. See [Add products](/guide/create/products).

## During a drop

### Can I pause a drop?

Only a queue—open the queue card's menu and select **Pause queue**, then **Resume queue** when you're ready to restart. Setting **Admit rate** to `0` also stops new admissions, but the queue won't display a **Paused** badge and **Resume queue** won't restore your previous rate. **Deactivate** isn't a pause—it ends the drop permanently. See [Change things mid-drop](/guide/drop/live-controls) and [Things that cannot be undone](/guide/reference/cannot-be-undone).

### What can I still change once a drop is live?

Most settings stay editable, and saving changes on a live distribution reaches customers immediately with no confirmation dialog. A few settings lock as the distribution opens—including payment collection, auction prices and quantity, and moving the start time later (queues count as open about 5.5 minutes early). To change a locked setting, you'll need to deactivate and create a replacement. Because a sequence or experience whose distributions are all deactivated won't accept new ones, you may need a new sequence or experience. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when).

### How long do customers have to pay?

Queues and timed releases give shoppers 30 minutes, and auctions give winners 30 minutes from winning. Draws use their **Admission expiration** setting (60 minutes by default if left blank on creation). On Shopify, customers check out in Shopify directly, so Fanfare's **Payment collection** setting doesn't apply. See [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) and [Limits and defaults](/guide/reference/limits).

### If I deactivate one sequence, what happens to the rest?

Your other sequences keep running normally, but a published hosted page marks the entire experience as ended—including for still-active sequences—with no way to keep the page open for them. Only organization admins can deactivate drops; on Shopify, anyone with access to the Fanfare app can. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) and [Things that cannot be undone](/guide/reference/cannot-be-undone).

## After a drop

### Why can't I delete my experience?

You can't delete an experience while anything inside it is **Scheduled** or **Live** (a live auction's Delete action routes you to Deactivate first). After deactivating, **Delete** remains blocked until active payment windows wind down plus about an hour—roughly 90 minutes at default settings, or about 2 hours for a draw—even though the button appears active. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).

### Why is Gross revenue higher than what I was paid?

**Gross revenue** totals every order, including unpaid orders (**Pending payment**, **Payment failed**, and **Cancelled**). Refunds are shown separately under **Refunded** rather than subtracted, and fees aren't deducted. To exclude unpaid orders, filter **Order status** to **Paid**, then to **Partially refunded**, and read the summary cards for each—note that those cards still reflect pre-refund totals. For collected revenue minus refunds, check **Net revenue** on **Performance**—your actual payout is lower by the Fanfare fee and Stripe's processing fees. See [Orders and refunds](/guide/learn/orders) and [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments).

### Does a refund put the item back in stock?

Only if you select **Return to stock**, and only for items whose stock Fanfare manages. Refunds are sent to Stripe as soon as you submit them and can't be undone. See [Orders and refunds](/guide/learn/orders) and [Things that cannot be undone](/guide/reference/cannot-be-undone).

## Plans and payments

### What happens to my audiences and pages if I downgrade?

Everything you've already set up keeps serving customers—live hosted pages stay published, and audiences continue gating access to your sequences. You just won't be able to create new higher-tier features or edit existing ones until you return to a plan that includes them. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).

### Why is Auction locked when my plan includes it?

If the **Auction** card says "Needs a checkout that charges the winning bid", that restriction comes from your checkout processor—not your plan—so upgrading won't unlock it. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans) and [Checkout options](/concepts/checkout-options).

### Can I change my organization's country or currency?

Not after creating or connecting a Stripe account—both lock permanently from that point on. You also can't change your currency while any undeleted auction exists. Because Fanfare checkout requires USD and a US Stripe account, set **Currency** to USD before connecting Stripe. See [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments) and [Organization settings](/guide/account/organization).

### What happens when my trial ends?

If you haven't added a payment card when your trial ends, your subscription cancels and your organization moves to **Starter**. Everything you've built stays intact and keeps serving customers, though creating or editing Growth features locks until you choose a paid plan. See [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing).

## On Shopify

### Can I refund a Shopify order in Fanfare?

No. Fanfare doesn't offer Refund or Cancel actions for Shopify orders—process refunds and cancellations directly in Shopify. Updates made in Shopify don't modify Fanfare's copy of the order. See [Orders with Shopify checkout](/guide/shopify/orders).

### Is my product still protected after the drop?

Yes. Checkout protection stays active after a drop ends—and even after you **Deactivate** it—until no experience includes the product. To let shoppers buy it normally again, delete every experience containing the product or remove the product from each one. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
