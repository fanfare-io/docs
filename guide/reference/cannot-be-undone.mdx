---
title: Things that cannot be undone
description: Every irreversible action and permanent setting lock in Fanfare, plus temporary locks that clear automatically.
---

Most actions in Fanfare take effect as soon as you confirm them, with no undo button. When a drop setting locks permanently, the only way to change it is to **Deactivate** and create a replacement—and **Deactivate** can't be undone either. Because a sequence or experience whose distributions are all deactivated won't accept new ones, you may need a new sequence or experience (see [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when)).

Some locks are temporary and lift on their own—see [Locks that lift](#locks-that-lift). For live edits that take effect right away but can be changed back manually (such as brand updates or **Event Points**), see [Change things mid-drop](/guide/drop/live-controls).

## Actions you cannot undo

| Action | What's permanent | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deactivate** a distribution, sequence, or experience | Can't be undone, even on one that already closed. Once every distribution in a sequence or experience is deactivated, you can't add new ones, and deactivating any part marks the entire hosted page as ended. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| **Delete** an experience, sequence, or distribution | Can't be restored. Orders, entries, and customer records are kept. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate#delete) |
| **Cancel booking** on an appointment | Can't be undone, and the customer is notified automatically. | [Change things mid-drop](/guide/drop/live-controls#cancel-a-booking) |
| **Submit refund** on an order | Sent to Stripe immediately and can't be undone. The amount defaults to the full remaining balance. | [Orders and refunds](/guide/learn/orders) |
| **Archive** a customer | Can't be restored, and their email address and phone number stay reserved. | [Customer profiles](/guide/learn/customers) |
| **Start Import** of a customer CSV | Overwrites every populated field on matched customers, replaces their metadata, and clears SMS consent if a phone number changes. Can't be undone. | [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences) |
| **Delete** an audience | Can't be restored. | [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences) |
| **Delete** a product | Can't be restored. | [Add products](/guide/create/products) |
| Remove an image from a product | Deleted immediately, with no confirmation dialog and no undo. | [Add products](/guide/create/products) |
| **Delete** a location | Can't be undone. Any appointment still using it can no longer be booked. | [Locations and opening hours](/guide/create/locations) |
| **Send code** to a waitlist | Sends right away to everyone on the waitlist and can't be undone. | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| **Delete** an email or text announcement, or cancel a social post or kit | Can't be undone or restored. | [Announcements](/guide/create/announcements) |
| **Archive page** on a published hosted page | Can't be undone. The URL stops working within about a minute, and any new page gets a new URL. | [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) |
| **Delete** a design in a hosted page's **Unused designs** list | Can't be undone. | [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) |
| **Reset poll** on a live page | Resets every vote count to zero and can't be undone. | [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) |
| **Delete** a design in Design Studio | Can't be undone from the admin. Anything already using the design keeps it. | [Templates and widget designs](/guide/create/templates) |
| **Rotate key** for the secret key | Can't be undone. The old key stops working within a minute, and the new key is shown only once. | [API keys and embedding](/guide/account/developer) |
| **Create account** or connect your own Stripe account | Permanently ties your organization to that Stripe account and locks your country and currency. | [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments) |
| **Disconnect** Stripe | Stops Fanfare checkout immediately. You can only reconnect the same Stripe account. | [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments) |
| **Cancel immediately** on a paid plan | Ends your paid plan right away with no refund for the rest of the current billing period. | [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing) |
| Switch Loyalty off on a plan below Pro | One-way until you upgrade back to Pro. | [Run a loyalty program](/guide/learn/loyalty) |
| Change **Experience Subdomain** | The old subdomain keeps serving with no end date, and no other organization can claim it. | [Organization settings](/guide/account/organization) |

## Settings fixed for good

| Setting | Locks when | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A product's **Stock management** | The product is created | [Add products](/guide/create/products) |
| Sequence priority | The sequence is created—follows creation order and can't be changed in the admin | [Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences), [Sequences](/concepts/sequences) |
| **Auction Type** | The auction is created | [Set up an auction](/guide/create/auction) |
| An auction's currency | The auction is created | [Auction](/concepts/distributions/auction) |
| Queue **Payment collection** and **Authorization amount** | About 5.5 minutes before **Opens** | [Set up a queue](/guide/create/queue) |
| Draw **Payment collection**, **Authorization amount**, and **Continue selecting until completed** | About 30 seconds before **Opens** | [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw) |
| Draw **Winner count** | Entry closes | [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw) |
| Auction prices and **Quantity** | About 30 seconds before **Opens** | [Set up an auction](/guide/create/auction) |
| **Opens** | About 30 seconds before **Opens** (about 5.5 minutes for a queue)—can only move earlier, and once closed the full schedule is final | [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) |
| Every setting on a deactivated distribution | You select **Deactivate** (becomes read-only) | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| Hosted page address | The page is first published (you can't customize the URL slug) | [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) |
| Organization country and currency | A Stripe account is created or connected, even after **Disconnect** | [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments) |
| A verified sending domain | Verification completes—can't be removed or replaced in the admin while showing **Verified** or **Needs attention** | [Sender identity and sending domain](/guide/create/email-sender) |
| A loyalty tier group's attribute | The group is created | [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences) |
| Start value of a tier group's lowest tier | The group is created—you can't type a new one, though adding a tier or removing the lowest one resets it to the lowest value in your customer data | [Loyalty and Fanfair](/concepts/loyalty) |
| A product with units sold in one of the experience's sequences | Its first sale in any sequence of the experience—it can't be removed from the experience | [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) |
| A released sequence's stock | The sequence releases after its drop ends | [Stock](/concepts/stock) |

For the stage-by-stage timeline of these locks and why they begin before opening, see [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when).

## Locks that lift

| What's locked | Stays locked while | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An appointment's slot settings, window, location, and time zone | Any booking stands | [Set up appointments](/guide/create/appointment) |
| A location's opening hours, time zone, and **Delete** | Any bookings stand at that location | [Locations and opening hours](/guide/create/locations) |
| A sequence's **Product Selection** and Mystery Box products | A distribution is open, has entrants or active checkouts, or stock counting has started (about 10 minutes before opening) | [Add and gate sequences](/guide/create/sequences) |
| An experience's products | Any of its sequences is locked | [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences) |
| An experience's stock allocations | The drop is live—sequence allocations can only increase once counting starts, about 10 minutes before opening | [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation) |
| Republishing a live hosted page (unless you type `PUBLISH` and select **Override freeze and publish**) | From 15 minutes before a distribution opens until it closes (or 30 minutes after opening if it has no close time) | [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page) |
| A **Dynamic** audience (**Refresh now** blocked and rule edits wait) | The audience is **Held** | [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| **Checkout processor** | Any distribution is scheduled or running, or winners can still pay. Switching to **External** also waits until no sequence offers a product choice and no Fanfare-managed product exists. | [Organization settings](/guide/account/organization) |
| **Currency** | Any auction exists, or an ended auction's winner can still pay | [Organization settings](/guide/account/organization) |
| **Delete** after a drop ends | Admissions wind down—the payment window plus about 1 hour after **Deactivate** (about 90 minutes, or about 2 hours for a draw at its default payment window), and longer when a drop ends on its own. An English auction that closed without settling is held about 30 days unless you deactivate it. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| Plan changes during a trial | Until you add a payment card | [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing) |
| Creating and editing Growth or Pro features after downgrading | Until you return to a plan that includes them (existing drops keep serving customers) | [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans) |

## On Shopify

- **No appointments or Fanfare payment locks:** The Shopify app doesn't include appointments, and shoppers pay in Shopify checkout, so payment collection locks don't apply. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when#on-shopify).
- **Deactivation permissions:** Everyone with access to the Fanfare app in Shopify counts as an organization admin and can deactivate drops. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) and [Team members](/guide/account/team).
- **Disconnecting Stripe:** If you previously connected a Stripe account and select **Disconnect**, you can't reconnect it from inside the Shopify app. See [Payments and fees](/guide/account/payments).
- **Uninstalling the app:** Moves your organization to **Starter** (canceled). See [Install and choose a plan](/guide/shopify/install-and-plans).
- **Shopify order changes:** Refund or cancel Shopify orders directly in Shopify—those updates don't change Fanfare's copy of the order. See [Orders with Shopify checkout](/guide/shopify/orders).
- **Checkout protection after a drop:** A product stays protected after its drop ends, even after **Deactivate**, until no experience includes it. Delete each experience holding the product or remove the product from each one. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
