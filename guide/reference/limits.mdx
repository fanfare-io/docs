Use this page to check a number without reading the whole page about a setting. Each row links to the page that explains it, where the field reference table says what the setting does. What locks at each moment is on [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when), and actions you cannot take back are on [Things that cannot be undone](/guide/reference/cannot-be-undone).

Where the new-experience wizard and **Add distribution** start a setting differently, both are given.

## Timing around an opening

| What | Default or limit | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queue waiting room | Opens 5 minutes before **Opens**. Nobody is let in before **Opens**. | [Queue](/concepts/distributions/queue) |
| When a queue, draw or auction counts as opened, and its locks start | 30 seconds before **Opens**. For a queue, 5 minutes 30 seconds before **Opens**. An appointment locks while bookings stand instead. | [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) |
| Stock counting | Starts about 10 minutes before a sequence opens | [Stock](/concepts/stock) |
| Pre-open audience rebuild | 5 minutes before the first distribution in the sequence starts taking entries. For a queue, 10 minutes before **Opens**. | [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| Daily rebuild of a **Dynamic** audience | 24 hours after its last build | [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| Hosted page publish freeze | From 15 minutes before any distribution in the experience opens until it closes. A distribution with no close time freezes publishing until 30 minutes after it opens. | [Brand, templates and designs](/concepts/design) |
| Waitlist "opens soon" message | 5 minutes before each opening, except for appointments | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| First invite email | 2 days before the sequence opens. About 30 minutes after set-up if the sequence opens sooner. | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| **Keep inviting** | **Every 15 minutes** | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| An opening time in the past | Accepted on create if it is up to 5 minutes ago; anything earlier is refused | [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences) |
| Opening and closing times | Saved to the whole minute | [Set up a timed release](/guide/create/timed-release) |
| An announcement moved into the past | Sends straight away if the new time is less than 24 hours ago. Never sends if it is 24 hours or more ago. | [Announcements](/guide/create/announcements) |

## Payment windows

The payment window is the time a customer has to pay once they get through. How it works is on [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).

| What | Default or limit | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queue | 30 minutes. You cannot change it. | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |
| Draw: **Admission expiration** | 60 minutes if left blank when you create the draw. 30 minutes if you clear the field later. | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |
| Draw: a short window when customers choose their product | Under 5 minutes shows a warning in **Add distribution** and **Edit**; you can still save | [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw) |
| Timed release | 30 minutes | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |
| Auction | 30 minutes from the win | [Auction](/concepts/distributions/auction) |
| Pre-authorised card holds (queue and draw) | Last about a week. A schedule is refused when a hold placed at opening would expire before the first selection (**Closes**, for a queue) plus the payment window. Holds placed early can also lapse before later selections, so keep the time from opening to the last selection, plus the payment window, under six days. | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |

### Auction

| Auto-extend (English) | Off. In the wizard, 5 minutes when you switch it on, chosen from 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes; elsewhere, a number of seconds. It extends **Settles** once, and never past **Closes**. | [Set up an auction](/guide/create/auction) |

## Delete after Deactivate

**Delete** stays unavailable after a distribution ends until customers who were let in can no longer pay, plus a margin.

| What | Default or limit | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After **Deactivate** | The payment window plus about an hour: about 90 minutes for a queue, timed release or auction, and about 2 hours for a draw whose **Admission expiration** was left blank when it was created. An appointment has no wait once its bookings are cancelled. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| A queue or timed release that closes on its own | Its payment window plus about an hour from its close: about 90 minutes | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| A draw that closes on its own | Its payment window plus about 2 hours from its close, or from its final selection if it keeps drawing until every unit is claimed: about 3 hours with a 60-minute **Admission expiration** | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| An auction that closes on its own | About 90 minutes from its close or settlement, whichever is later | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| An English auction that closed without settling | About 30 days, unless you deactivate it, which brings it down to about 90 minutes | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |

## On Shopify

In the Fanfare app in Shopify:

- Products come in through Shopify's product picker, up to 20 at a time. An experience still holds at most 10. See [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products).
- An imported Shopify order can have at most 250 lines. See [Orders with Shopify checkout](/guide/shopify/orders).
- The Fanfare block on a product in Shopify admin stops waiting after about 10 seconds and shows its empty state. See [Fanfare inside Shopify admin](/guide/shopify/in-shopify-admin).
- Customers pay in Shopify's checkout, so there is no **Payment collection** choice and the pre-authorisation rows do not apply. See [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).
- Appointments are not offered, so the appointment rows do not apply.
- You approve a plan on Shopify's plan page. See [Install and choose a plan](/guide/shopify/install-and-plans).
