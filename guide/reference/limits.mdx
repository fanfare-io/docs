Use this page to check any default, limit, or timing window at a glance. Each row links to the guide that explains how the setting works in full. For when settings lock, see [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when); for irreversible actions, see [Things that cannot be undone](/guide/reference/cannot-be-undone).

When the experience wizard and **Add distribution** use different defaults, both are listed below.

## Timing around an opening

| Setting or event | Default or limit | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queue waiting room | Opens 5 minutes before **Opens**. No one is admitted before **Opens**. | [Queue](/concepts/distributions/queue) |
| When a queue, draw, or auction counts as opened (and locks start) | 30 seconds before **Opens** (5 minutes 30 seconds before **Opens** for a queue). Appointments lock while bookings stand instead. | [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when) |
| Stock counting | Starts about 10 minutes before a sequence opens. | [Stock](/concepts/stock) |
| Pre-open audience rebuild | 5 minutes before the sequence's first distribution starts accepting entries (10 minutes before **Opens** for a queue). | [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| Daily rebuild of a **Dynamic** audience | 24 hours after its last build. | [Audiences and access codes](/concepts/audiences) |
| Hosted page publish freeze | From 15 minutes before any distribution in the experience opens until it closes (or 30 minutes after opening if it has no close time). | [Brand, templates and designs](/concepts/design) |
| Waitlist "opens soon" message | 5 minutes before each opening (doesn't apply to appointments). | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| First invite email | 2 days before the sequence opens, or about 30 minutes after setup if the sequence opens sooner. | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| **Keep inviting** | **Every 15 minutes**. | [Invites, waitlist and access-code sends](/guide/create/invites-and-waitlist) |
| Opening time in the past | Accepted on creation if it's up to 5 minutes ago; anything earlier is rejected. | [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences) |
| Opening and closing times | Saved to the whole minute. | [Set up a timed release](/guide/create/timed-release) |
| Announcement moved into the past | Sends immediately if the new time is less than 24 hours ago. Won't send if it's 24 hours or more ago. | [Announcements](/guide/create/announcements) |

## Payment windows

The payment window is how long an admitted customer has to pay before losing their spot. See [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) for details.

| Setting or distribution | Default or limit | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queue | 30 minutes (can't be changed). | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |
| Draw (**Admission expiration**) | 60 minutes if left blank on creation; 30 minutes if cleared later. | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |
| Draw short-window warning (when customers pick their product) | Under 5 minutes displays a warning in **Add distribution** and **Edit**, though you can still save. | [Set up a draw](/guide/create/draw) |
| Timed release | 30 minutes. | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |
| Auction | 30 minutes from the win. | [Auction](/concepts/distributions/auction) |
| Pre-authorized card holds (queue and draw) | Expire after about 7 days. Schedules are rejected if a hold placed at opening would expire before the first selection (**Closes** for a queue) plus the payment window. Keep the span from opening to final selection, plus the payment window, under 6 days so early holds don't lapse. | [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment) |

## Delete after Deactivate

**Delete** stays unavailable after a distribution ends until admitted customers can no longer pay, plus a short buffer.

| Scenario | Wait time | Explained in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After **Deactivate** | Payment window plus about 1 hour—roughly 90 minutes for a queue, timed release, or auction, and about 2 hours for a draw whose **Admission expiration** was left blank on creation. Appointments have no wait once bookings are canceled. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| Queue or timed release that closes on its own | Payment window plus about 1 hour from close (about 90 minutes). | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| Draw that closes on its own | Payment window plus about 2 hours from close—or from its final selection if it keeps drawing until all units are claimed (about 3 hours with a 60-minute **Admission expiration**). | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| Auction that closes on its own | About 90 minutes from close or settlement, whichever is later. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |
| English auction that closed without settling | About 30 days—unless you deactivate it, which lowers the wait to about 90 minutes. | [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate) |

## On Shopify

In the Fanfare app in Shopify:

- **Product imports:** Add products through Shopify's product picker up to 20 at a time (each experience still holds up to 10 products). See [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products).
- **Order line limit:** Imported Shopify orders support up to 250 line items. See [Orders with Shopify checkout](/guide/shopify/orders).
- **Shopify admin product block:** The Fanfare block on a Shopify product page times out after about 10 seconds and displays its empty state. See [Fanfare inside Shopify admin](/guide/shopify/in-shopify-admin).
- **No pre-authorization:** Customers pay in Shopify checkout, so **Payment collection** and pre-authorization limits don't apply. See [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).
- **No appointments:** Appointments aren't offered in the Shopify app, so appointment defaults don't apply.
- **Plan approval:** Approve and manage your plan on Shopify's plan page. See [Install and choose a plan](/guide/shopify/install-and-plans).
