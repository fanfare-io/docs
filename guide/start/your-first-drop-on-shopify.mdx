title: Your first drop on Shopify
description: The steps, in order, to run your first Fanfare drop on a product in your Shopify store, from installing the app to checking that the product is protected.
---

This page walks you through your first drop on a Shopify store, from installing the Fanfare app to the day after the drop. The Fanfare app in Shopify has no separate sign-up: installing it from Shopify creates your Fanfare organisation. Each step below says what to do and links to the page that covers it in full, with its settings and what can go wrong.

## Before you start

- Your store's currency is one Fanfare can price. If it is not, the app shows only a message asking you to change it: see [Install and choose a plan](/guide/shopify/install-and-plans).
- You can install apps on the store, and change its checkout settings and its published theme in Shopify admin.

## 1. Install the app and choose a plan

Install the Fanfare app from Shopify and approve the permissions it asks for. Fanfare creates your organisation automatically, in your store's currency. Before you can use the app, you approve a plan on Shopify's plan page, Starter included; after you approve it you may see **Confirming your plan** for a moment while Fanfare checks with Shopify. See [Install and choose a plan](/guide/shopify/install-and-plans).

Everyone on your store's staff who can open the Fanfare app can change every Fanfare setting, including API keys and the email sender: see [Who can use the app](/guide/shopify/overview#who-can-use-the-app).

## 2. Set up your storefront

Once the app opens, Home (the page headed **Dashboard**) shows "Finish setting up Fanfare" with how many of its two steps are done, such as "1 of 2 done", and the "Setting up Fanfare on your store" tour walks you through the same two steps. Both are done on **Settings → Storefront setup**.

- **Turn on checkout protection.** Fanfare usually turns this on for you at install. Check that the **Checkout protection** card shows **On**.
- **Add the product widget to your theme.** Select **Add to product page**. The theme editor opens with the Fanfare Product Widget block ready to place: drag it to where your buy buttons sit, then save. If your store uses more than one product template, add it to every template that sells a product you will drop.

When both are done, Home shows "Fanfare is set up on your store".

Your drop does not work until both steps are done: checkout protection blocks every other way to buy the product, and the widget is how customers get in. [Storefront setup](/guide/shopify/storefront-setup) has the full steps in Shopify admin, and the settings that can quietly leave protection off.

## 3. Create the experience on your product

An experience is the drop you are running: the products on offer, the time zone, and every way customers can get in. On Shopify you do not create the product in Fanfare first. You pick it from your Shopify catalogue inside the experience wizard, so bringing in the product and creating the experience are one step.

1. Open **Experiences** and select **Create Experience**. You can also start from the product's page in Shopify admin: choose **Create Fanfare experience** from the page's actions and then **Create in Fanfare**, or, while the product has no experience yet, select **Start a Fanfare experience** in the **Fanfare experiences** block. The wizard then opens with the product already attached. See [Fanfare inside Shopify admin](/guide/shopify/in-shopify-admin).
2. On **Experience type**, choose **Queue**, **Draw**, **Auction** or **Timed Release**. Appointment is not offered in the Fanfare app in Shopify. **Auction** stays locked with "Needs a checkout that charges the winning bid" unless your store's checkout can charge the winning bid: see [Auctions on Shopify](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection#auctions-on-shopify). For a first drop, a [queue](/guide/create/queue) or a [timed release](/guide/create/timed-release) is the simplest choice.
3. On **Configure Experience**, pick your product. Shopify's product picker opens: you pick whole products, not single variants, and Fanfare copies each one in with all its variants. An experience can hold up to 10 products. Product details that come from Shopify are read-only in Fanfare; after you change a product in Shopify, select **Sync from Shopify** on its page in Fanfare to bring the change in at once. See [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products).

   <Warning>
     Your product is protected from the moment you create the experience, whatever its status, and it stays protected after the drop ends, even if you deactivate the experience. Customers cannot buy it the normal way, from the product page or through a cart link, until you delete the experience or remove the product from it, and neither is always possible straight after the drop. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
   </Warning>

4. Enter an **Experience Name**. Set **Time Zone** first, then **Opens At** and **Closes At**.

   <Note>
     **Opens At** starts at the next minute. If you leave it, the drop opens almost as soon as you create it, so set the opening time before you go on. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).
   </Note>

5. On **Stock**, set how many units this drop can sell. Shopify keeps the count for a product that comes from Shopify. See [Allocate stock to a drop](/guide/create/stock-allocation).
6. Complete the steps for your type. For a queue or a draw, the **Payment** step has nothing to choose: customers pay in Shopify checkout, so Fanfare collects no payment. A timed release has no **Payment** step.
7. On **Access**, choose who can enter. On **Review & launch**, check each row, then select **Create experience**.

If a message that stays on screen says Shopify gating could not be updated for a product, that product may not be protected: see [When protection did not update](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection#when-protection-did-not-update).

## 4. Make it look right

The widget's look comes from Fanfare, not from the Shopify theme editor, so every experience on your store matches. You can set your brand's colours and fonts on any plan; choosing widget designs in **Design Studio** needs Growth or above. If you set no brand font, the widget uses your theme's font. See [Set your brand](/guide/create/brand) and [Templates and widget designs](/guide/create/templates).

A hosted page is optional, and is available on Growth and above: see [Build and publish the hosted page](/guide/create/hosted-page). A hosted page cannot hand customers to Shopify checkout, so customers who are let in there see no checkout button. Run the drop on your product page.

To see what the widget replaces on your product page before the drop, use the preview in the theme editor: see [Storefront setup](/guide/shopify/storefront-setup).

## 5. Tell customers

Save a postal address in **Settings → Email Sender** first: Fanfare will not save or schedule an email announcement without it. See [Sender identity and sending domain](/guide/create/email-sender). Then schedule your announcements from the experience's **Marketing Hub**: see [Announcements](/guide/create/announcements).

Test sends go to your own Shopify staff account's email address, and only when Shopify has verified that address.

## 6. Confirm the drop is protected

Before doors open, run the three checks in [Check that a drop is protected](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection#check-that-a-drop-is-protected):

1. The **Checkout protection** card on **Settings → Storefront setup** shows **On**.
2. No "Shopify gating could not be updated" message appeared after you last saved the experience.
3. Optionally, a test checkout of the product that does not go through the widget, for example from a direct cart link, is refused.

## 7. Watch it

While the drop is live, follow it in the Fanfare app: see [Watch a live drop](/guide/drop/watch-live). To see the same moment from your customers' side, see [What your customers see](/guide/drop/what-customers-see).

{/* image: your-first-drop-on-shopify-widget-live */}

Paid orders from your experience reach **Orders** in the Fanfare app on their own. Cancelling or refunding an order in Shopify does not change it in Fanfare. See [Orders with Shopify checkout](/guide/shopify/orders).

## 8. After the drop

Your product stays protected until you delete the experience or remove the product from it. Neither is always possible straight away: you cannot remove a product while the experience still holds units of it, and after the drop ends, **Delete** waits for admissions to wind down. Plan for that if you want to sell it normally again: see [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection) and [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate). Your results are in [Read your results](/guide/learn/performance).
