When you sign in to [admin.fanfare.io](https://admin.fanfare.io), you land on **Home**. Everything in Fanfare lives in one of two places: the **sidebar** on the left for your day-to-day drops and settings, and the **user menu** at the bottom left for your team, profile, and billing.

{/* image: guide-start-tour-of-the-admin-home-and-sidebar */}

## The sidebar

Use the left sidebar to move between the main areas of your workspace:

| Section | What you'll manage here | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home** | Organization performance overview and the Experience Calendar. | This page |
| **Products** | Your catalog of products, variants, and stock. | [Add products](/guide/create/products) |
| **Experiences** | Your drops, along with their sequences and distributions. | [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences) |
| **Audiences** | Customer segments you can use to gate access to drops. | [Build audiences](/guide/create/audiences) |
| **Customers** | Profiles and activity history for customers who enter your drops. | [Customer profiles](/guide/learn/customers) |
| **Orders** | Paid orders and refunds. | [Orders and refunds](/guide/learn/orders) |
| **Loyalty** | Points, tiers, and loyalty program rules. | [Run a loyalty program](/guide/learn/loyalty) |
| **Design Studio** | Your brand settings, page templates, and widget designs. | [Set your brand](/guide/create/brand) |
| **Locations** | Physical stores and venues with opening hours for appointments. | [Locations and opening hours](/guide/create/locations) |
| **Settings** | Organization details, payments, integrations, and developer keys. | [Settings](#settings) below |

Every section stays visible in the sidebar regardless of your plan. If a specific feature requires a higher plan, you'll see an upgrade prompt on that page rather than a locked navigation item. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).

## The user menu

Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the user menu:

- **Account** — Manage your organization's team members and invitations. See [Team members](/guide/account/team).
- **Profile** — Manage your personal account details.
- **Billing** — View or change your plan and payment method. See [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing).
- **Usage** — Review Fanfare's per-order fees on your paid orders.
- **Sign Out** — Sign out of the Fanfare admin.

## Home

**Home** (titled **Dashboard** at the top of the page) gives you a live snapshot of your organization's activity and upcoming schedule.

### Performance metrics

Choose a date range at the top—**Last 24 hours**, **7 days** (default), **30 days**, or **90 days**—to view five headline cards: **Views**, **Entries**, **Wins**, **Paid orders**, and **Net revenue**. Below the cards, a chart plots **Entries** over time, and you can switch it to **Views**, **Wins**, or **Paid orders**. See [Read your results](/guide/learn/performance) for metric definitions.

Metrics refresh automatically every 30 seconds while the tab is open. You can toggle **Auto-refresh On** to pause automatic updates or select **Refresh** to update on demand.

If there's no activity in the selected time range, the cards and chart display faded **Sample data** until real customer activity comes in. Until you create your first experience, **Home** also shows **Quick Actions** shortcuts for **Create Experience**, **Create Dynamic Audience**, and **Create Product**.

### Experience Calendar

Once you've created an experience, the **Experience Calendar** takes the place of Quick Actions. It displays one month at a time alongside a list of every drop running that month.

- Select a day on the calendar to jump to the drop running on it, or select any row in the list to open that experience.
- Use the **Status** filter (**All**, **Upcoming**, **Active**, **Closed**) to narrow the list and calendar markers.
- Deactivating an experience before it opens removes it from the calendar; deactivating it mid-drop marks it as **Closed**.

<Note>
  The calendar uses your computer's local time zone rather than your organization's default time zone, so late-evening drops can appear on adjacent days for teammates in other zones. Experiences appear on the calendar once they have both an opening and a closing time.
</Note>

## Guided tour and billing alerts

- **Onboarding tour:** The first time you sign in, Fanfare offers a quick walkthrough of key areas in the sidebar. You can replay it anytime from **Settings → Tours & Tips**.
- **Billing banners:** If your trial is ending soon, a payment fails, or a subscription cancellation is scheduled, a banner appears at the top of the app with a direct link to resolve it. See [Billing and usage](/guide/account/billing).

## On Shopify

Inside Shopify, Fanfare uses Shopify's left-hand navigation instead of its own sidebar and user menu:

- **Billing** and **Usage** appear directly as top-level items under the Fanfare app, and **Billing** opens Shopify's plan page.
- There is no **Account**, **Profile**, or **Sign Out** menu—access is managed through Shopify staff permissions.
- **Home** includes a storefront setup checklist, and **Quick Actions** shows **Create Location** instead of **Create Product** (since products come from your Shopify catalog).
- **Settings** adds **Storefront setup** (where you can also replay the storefront setup walkthrough) and omits **Tours & Tips**.

See [Fanfare in Shopify](/guide/shopify/overview) and [Storefront setup](/guide/shopify/storefront-setup) for more details.
