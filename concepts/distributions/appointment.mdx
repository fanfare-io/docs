An **appointment** lets customers reserve a scheduled time slot—ideal for in-store fittings, trunk shows, private viewings, pickups, or online consultations. You define the date range, slot length, buffer between slots, and capacity per slot; Fanfare generates the bookable schedule from your location's opening hours so shoppers can pick a time that works for them.

<Info>
  Available on **Growth** and above. On Starter, appointments appear grayed out with an upgrade link. Any appointment created on a higher plan continues running and remains editable if you downgrade later.
</Info>

## How appointments differ from product drops

While queues, draws, auctions, and timed releases allocate inventory, appointments reserve time:

- **No inventory allocated:** Appointments reserve time slots without assigning products or deducting stock.
- **No checkout or Fanfare fees:** Customers don't pay when booking, there's no payment step during setup, and Fanfare charges no per-order fees.
- **Sign-in required:** Guest access isn't supported. While an appointment is open or scheduled, its entire sequence requires shoppers to sign in—even if another distribution in that sequence allows guests. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
- **No waitlist:** Sequences running only appointments take bookings directly rather than maintaining a waitlist (the sequence has no **Waitlist** tab). To notify customers when booking opens, send an [announcement](/guide/create/announcements).

## How bookable slots are generated

Fanfare builds your slot schedule from three inputs:

- **Date window:** **First bookable slot** and **Closes** (**Closes At** in the experience wizard) bound which slots exist. Any slot that would start before **First bookable slot** or end after **Closes** is excluded.
- **Opening hours:**
  - **Physical Location** — Slots follow the location's opening hours in the location's time zone (even if the appointment's **Time Zone** differs). Closed days have no slots; locations without opening hours default to 09:00–17:00 daily.
  - **Online** — Slots run 09:00–17:00 daily in the appointment's **Time Zone**.
- **Slot sizing and capacity:** Starting at opening time, Fanfare lays out slots of **Slot duration**, separated by **Buffer**, stopping after the last full slot that fits before closing time. Each slot accepts up to **Max capacity per slot** bookings before showing as full.

<Note>
  **First bookable slot** controls which slots appear—not when customers can start booking. Customers can book as soon as your page goes live. To control when booking opens, control when you publish the page.
</Note>

See [Set up appointments](/guide/create/appointment) for every field and its limits, and [Locations and opening hours](/guide/create/locations) to configure store hours.

## Bookings lock your setup

As soon as the first customer books, **Slot duration**, **Buffer**, **Max capacity per slot**, the location, **First bookable slot**, **Closes**, and **Time Zone** lock. They stay locked as long as any active booking exists—a booking stops counting once its slot ends or it's canceled.

While any booking stands at a physical location, that location's opening hours and time zone lock too. See [Locations and opening hours](/guide/create/locations).

Two window rules apply regardless of bookings: once **First bookable slot** passes, you can move it earlier but not later, and once the appointment ends, its window can't change.

<Warning>
  Double-check your slot configuration and location hours before publishing your page. Once a customer books, changing slot settings requires waiting for all booked slots to end or canceling existing bookings—which notifies every affected customer and can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Booking experience and management

Signed-in customers pick a day and time, add the appointment to their calendar, and can hold **one active booking per appointment** at a time. They can reschedule anytime before the slot starts and cancel anytime before the slot ends.

Customers automatically receive confirmation, reschedule, and cancellation messages (see [What your customers see](/guide/drop/what-customers-see)). Fanfare doesn't send pre-slot reminder notifications.

From the sequence's **Overview** tab, you can cancel any individual booking for an upcoming slot within the next 7 days before it ends; the customer is notified automatically. See [Change things mid-drop](/guide/drop/live-controls). (Fanfare doesn't currently track check-ins or no-shows in the admin.)

## Monitoring upcoming bookings

On the sequence's **Overview** tab, the appointment panel displays a rolling 7-day strip (today plus the next 6 days) showing booked counts per day and the attendee list for each slot. Bookings further out appear automatically once their date enters that 7-day window. Times display in the location's time zone (or the appointment's **Time Zone** for online appointments), with days outside your schedule labeled **"not in window"** or **"closed"**.

## Lifecycle and deactivation

An appointment moves from **Scheduled** (labeled **Upcoming** on the **Overview** panel) until **First bookable slot**, to **Live** until **Closes**, and then to **Ended** with no settling phase. Customers can book while it's still **Scheduled** as long as your page is live.

Choosing **Deactivate** while scheduled or live sets the status to **Ended early**, cancels every unended booking, and sends each affected customer a cancellation notice. Deactivating can't be undone; see [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).

Like all distributions, an appointment can't overlap another distribution in the same sequence. See [Distributions](/concepts/distributions/overview).

## Example: a trunk show at the SoHo Flagship

Northbound Supply Co. runs the **Aether Loafer Trunk Show** at their SoHo Flagship location with these settings:

- **Slot duration:** 30 minutes
- **Buffer:** 10 minutes
- **Max capacity per slot:** 2

SoHo Flagship is open 10:00–18:00 Monday through Saturday and closed Sunday. With a 30-minute slot and 10-minute buffer, each slot starts 40 minutes after the previous one:

| Slot | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10:00 | 10:30 |
| 2 | 10:40 | 11:10 |
| 3 | 11:20 | 11:50 |
| 4 | 12:00 | 12:30 |
| 5 | 12:40 | 13:10 |
| 6 | 13:20 | 13:50 |
| 7 | 14:00 | 14:30 |
| 8 | 14:40 | 15:10 |
| 9 | 15:20 | 15:50 |
| 10 | 16:00 | 16:30 |
| 11 | 16:40 | 17:10 |
| 12 | 17:20 | 17:50 |

A slot starting at 18:00 would run past closing, so each open day yields 12 slots with 2 spots each (24 fittings per day), and Sunday has none.

With **First bookable slot** set to Thursday at 14:00 and **Closes** set to the following Thursday at 12:00, slots 1–6 are excluded on the first Thursday, and slots 4–12 are excluded on the final Thursday (since slot 4 finishes after 12:00).

When the page goes live Monday and the first customer books Thursday's 14:00 slot on Tuesday, both the appointment setup and SoHo Flagship's opening hours lock. Switching to 45-minute fittings requires waiting until every booked slot passes or canceling those bookings first.

## On Shopify

Appointments aren't available in the Fanfare Shopify app on any plan, and they can't be added in the Fanfare admin to an experience backed by a Shopify product. Because Shopify products can only be purchased through their experience—and appointments don't sell products—attaching an appointment would leave that Shopify product unbuyable. To run appointments from the Fanfare admin, create a product directly in Fanfare for that experience instead.
