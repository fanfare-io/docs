An **auction** lets demand determine the final price—ideal for one-of-a-kind, numbered, or high-value releases where you'd rather not fix a retail price up front. Available on every Fanfare plan (including Starter), auctions come in two formats chosen at creation:

- **English (ascending)** — Shoppers bid against each other as the price climbs. When bidding ends, the highest bidder wins.
- **Dutch (descending)** — The price starts high and steps down on a schedule. Shoppers can buy immediately at the current price until stock sells out or the auction settles.

You can't switch between English and Dutch after creating an auction.

## How the schedule works

Every auction has three timestamps: **Opens**, **Settles** (labeled **Settle at** when adding an auction to a sequence), and **Closes**.

- **Opens to Settles:** Bidding (English) or buying (Dutch) is open.
- **Settles:** Bidding closes—the highest bidder wins in an English auction, and the price freezes in a Dutch auction.
- **Settles to Closes:** The auction is in its settling period.

An English winner gets **30 minutes to pay** starting when bidding ends and the result is confirmed—**Closes** neither shortens nor extends that window. If the winner doesn't pay in time, they see **"Payment window closed"**, lose the item, and it isn't offered to the runner-up.

Like all distributions, the window from **Opens** to **Closes** can't overlap another distribution in the same sequence. See [Distributions](/concepts/distributions/overview).

## English auctions

Each new bid must exceed the current highest bid by at least the **Minimum Bid Increment**. You can also set an optional **Reserve Price**—the lowest price you're willing to accept. If the highest bid at **Settles** falls below the reserve, nobody wins and all bids are released.

On a stock-counted sequence, an English auction offers one unit of each available size and the winner takes one (so there's no quantity field; unsold units remain in the sequence). If the sequence has no stock, the auction opens sold out. A shopper's first bid locks their chosen item or size.

### Preventing last-second sniping

Enable **Auto-Extend** (**Auto Extend Seconds** in auction settings) to prevent last-second bids from winning unanswered. If a bid lands within the extension window before **Settles**, the settle time pushes back by that duration:

- **Triggers once:** A second late bid during the extension won't extend the auction again.
- **Never passes Closes:** If an extension would run past **Closes**, bidding ends at **Closes**.
- **Schedule room required:** **Settles** must be scheduled at least the extension duration before **Closes**.

## Dutch auctions

A Dutch auction opens at the **Start Price** and drops by the **Price Drop Amount** every **Price Drop Interval (seconds)** until it reaches the **Floor Price**. Shoppers claim a unit immediately at the live price by selecting **"Buy now — [amount]"**.

On a stock-counted sequence, available units equal the total across your sequence sizes (adjust counts on the sequence's **Stock** card). Otherwise, set the unit count with **Quantity**.

Every Dutch buyer has **30 minutes to pay** from the moment they buy:

- If a buyer doesn't pay in time while the auction is still live, their unit returns to the sale at the current price.
- Once the auction ends—or sells out its last unit in a **Quantity**-based Dutch auction—any lapsed unit stays unsold.

At **Settles**, the price freezes and buying stops; any remaining units stay unsold. Reaching the **Floor Price** doesn't end the auction early—shoppers can keep buying at the floor until **Settles**. If units remain unsold at the floor when the auction ends, shoppers who didn't buy see **"Unsold"** (**"The auction reached its floor price before all items were claimed."**).

## Pricing locks when the auction opens

<Warning>
  Once an auction opens, pricing is locked. You can't edit **Reserve Price**, **Minimum Bid Increment**, **Start Price**, **Floor Price**, **Price Drop Amount**, **Price Drop Interval (seconds)**, or **Quantity** after **Opens**. You also can't push **Opens** later once live to reopen pricing. **Auto Extend Seconds** stays editable.
</Warning>

## Rules specific to auctions

- **Sign-in required:** Guest access isn't supported. While an auction is open or scheduled, its entire sequence requires shoppers to sign in—even if other distributions in the sequence allow guests. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
- **No automatic entry:** Shoppers always choose to place a bid or buy.
- **Payment after winning:** No card hold is placed when bidding.
- **Binding bids:** Once a customer places a bid, they can't withdraw it or leave the auction.
- **Currency locked at creation:** Auctions use your organization's currency at creation time. You can't change your organization's currency while any undeleted auction or active winner checkout window exists. See [Organization settings](/guide/account/organization).

### Checkout requirements

Because the final price isn't known until bidding ends, auctions require either Fanfare checkout or a connected checkout capable of charging the exact winning bid in your organization's currency. Without a compatible checkout, **Auction** appears locked with **"Needs a checkout that charges the winning bid"**.

This is a checkout capability requirement, not a plan restriction—upgrading your Fanfare plan won't unlock it. See [Checkout options](/concepts/checkout-options).

## What your customers see

- **Live English ledger:** Displays the current highest bid, minimum next bid, and **"Sale needs at least"** until the reserve price is met.
- **High-bid confirmation:** Shoppers bidding well above the minimum next bid see a confirmation prompt showing what they'll pay if nobody outbids them.
- **Outbid alerts:** Outbid shoppers see **"Outbid — the highest bid is now [amount]"** and receive an email or SMS alert.
- **Settling and outcome:** After **Settles**, shoppers see **"Auction settling"**, followed by **"Won at [amount]"** (English) or **"Bought at [amount]"** (Dutch) for winners. If an English auction ends below its reserve price, customers simply see **"Ended"** (the unmet reserve isn't disclosed).

See [What your customers see](/guide/drop/what-customers-see) for the complete customer journey.

## Deactivating an auction

**Deactivate** voids active bids so no winner is selected. A winner who has already started checkout keeps their item and can still pay, while any other winner loses their spot. After bidding ends, an English auction can block deactivation while its winner's checkout window is still open, and an auction that ended without settling must be deactivated on its own. See [Stop a drop](/guide/drop/deactivate).

To watch bids come in live, open the auction panel on your sequence's **Overview** tab. See [Watch a live drop](/guide/drop/watch-live).

## Example: auctioning a numbered jacket

Northbound Supply Co. auctions a single numbered Eternal Bloom Silk Bomber using an English auction:

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Reserve Price** | `650.00` |
| **Minimum Bid Increment** | `25.00` |
| **Settles** | 18:00 |
| **Closes** | 19:00 |
| **Auto Extend Seconds** | `300` (5 minutes) |

1. Early bids come in at `600.00` and `625.00`. Because the reserve hasn't been met yet, the ledger displays **"Sale needs at least"** `650.00`.
2. At 17:40, a bidder enters `650.00` and meets the reserve.
3. At 17:58, another shopper bids `675.00`. Since that lands inside the final 5 minutes, **Settles** extends once from 18:00 to 18:05.
4. At 18:03, the previous leader bids `700.00`. Because the extension already triggered once, **Settles** stays at 18:05.
5. At 18:05, bidding closes and shoppers see **"Auction settling"**. The `700.00` bid wins and that shopper sees **"Won at 700.00"**.
6. The winner has 30 minutes (until ~18:35) to check out. If they don't pay in time, the jacket stays unsold—it isn't offered to the `675.00` bidder.

## On Shopify

In the Shopify app, your organization's currency automatically matches your store's currency.

With Shopify checkout, **Auction** stays locked with **"Needs a checkout that charges the winning bid"** unless all four conditions are met:

- Your store is on **Shopify Plus**.
- The **Fanfare checkout validation** rule is active and blocking. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
- The Fanfare app has all requested permissions.
- Your store currency matches your organization currency.

Fanfare re-checks these requirements on install, whenever your store or app permissions change, and each time you open the app in Shopify admin until every check passes.
