Fanfare is a platform for managing controlled product access through virtual waiting rooms, queues, auctions, draws, and appointments. This guide explains the key concepts you'll work with.

## How Fanfare Works

When you launch a high-demand product or event, Fanfare helps you manage the consumer experience by controlling who gets access, when, and how.

![Platform overview showing consumers entering a Fanfare experience with VIP, general, and waitlist paths before checkout.](/images/concepts/platform-overview.webp)

## Core Concepts

### Experiences

An **Experience** is your product launch, sale event, or access-controlled offering. Think of it as a campaign that groups together everything needed to manage consumer access.

Experiences define:

- **When it opens and closes** — Control exactly when consumers can participate
- **What products are available** — Connect your products to the experience
- **How consumers get access** — Through queues, draws, auctions, or other methods

[Learn more about Experiences →](/concepts/experiences)

### Distribution Types

A **Distribution** is the method that controls how consumers gain access to purchase. Fanfare supports several types:

| Type              | How It Works                  | Best For                         |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Queue**         | First-come, first-served line | High-demand launches             |
| **Draw**          | Random lottery selection      | Fair allocation of limited items |
| **Auction**       | Competitive bidding           | Unique or collectible items      |
| **Appointment**   | Scheduled time slots          | In-store pickups, consultations  |
| **Timed Release** | Instant access at a set time  | Flash sales, member perks        |
| **Waitlist**      | Notify when available         | Pre-launch interest, overflow    |

[Learn more about Distribution Types →](/concepts/distributions/overview)

### Consumers

Fanfare tracks your consumers as they participate in experiences. Consumers can be:

- **Guests** — Shopping without an account (guest checkout)
- **Registered** — Have provided email or phone
- **Verified** — Confirmed their identity via email or phone

As consumers engage, Fanfare calculates helpful scores:

- **Loyalty score** — Based on purchase history and engagement
- **Activity level** — Recent engagement patterns

[Learn more about Consumers →](/concepts/consumers)

### Audiences

An **Audience** is a group of consumers you want to target. Use audiences to create exclusive access for specific groups:

- **VIP consumers** — Based on purchase history or loyalty
- **Newsletter subscribers** — Synced from your email platform
- **Invite lists** — Manually curated for exclusive events

[Learn more about Audiences →](/concepts/audiences)

### Products

Connect your **Products** to experiences to make them available for purchase. Fanfare supports:

- **Variants** — Different sizes, colors, or options
- **Inventory tracking** — Optional stock management
- **Media assets** — Images and descriptions

[Learn more about Products →](/concepts/products)

### Instant Updates

Consumers see their status in real time:

- Queue position and estimated wait time
- Current high bid in auctions
- Draw entry confirmation
- Countdown to access

## Choosing the Right Approach

| Your Situation                                | Recommended Distribution | Why                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| More demand than supply, fairness matters     | **Queue**                | First-come ordering feels fair |
| Limited inventory, everyone deserves a chance | **Draw**                 | Random selection is equitable  |
| Unique items, price discovery needed          | **Auction**              | Bidding finds the right price  |
| Scheduled service or pickup                   | **Appointment**          | Time slots work best           |
| Quick access at a specific time               | **Timed Release**        | Simple, instant access         |
| Building interest before launch               | **Waitlist**             | Capture demand early           |
