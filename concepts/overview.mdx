Every drop in Fanfare is built from three nested pieces: an **experience**, its **sequences**, and their **distributions**.

An **experience** is your drop—the products on offer, the schedule, and the customer-facing design. Inside it, you set up one or more **sequences** to create distinct entry paths (such as a VIP early-access wave followed by a public release). Each sequence runs one or more **distributions**—a queue, draw, auction, timed release, or appointment—one window at a time. When a customer arrives, Fanfare automatically routes them into a single sequence and guides them through its entry gates and active distribution to the right to buy.

## How the pieces nest

| Layer | What it holds | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization** | Your plan, checkout processor, products, customer profiles, audiences, brand and templates, locations, and loyalty program. | Shared across drops (below) |
| **Experience** | Up to 10 products, a time zone, branding and hosted page (Growth and above), announcements, and one or more sequences. | [Experiences](/concepts/experiences) |
| **Sequence** | Optional audience and access-code gates, a dedicated share of stock, product assignment (**Customer Picks** or **Mystery Box**), order limits, a built-in waitlist, and a routing priority. | [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences) |
| **Distribution** | One of Fanfare's five release formats with its own **Opens** and **Closes** schedule. Distributions inside the same sequence run back-to-back, never overlapping. | [Distributions](/concepts/distributions/overview) |

Every sequence also comes with its own waitlist. A waitlist isn't a distribution and doesn't sell inventory—it captures early interest before a sequence opens. See [Waitlists](/concepts/waitlists).

When you run the **Create Experience** wizard, Fanfare sets up your experience, first sequence, and first distribution all at once. You can add more sequences and distributions afterward. See [Create an experience](/guide/create/experiences).

## How a customer moves through a drop

Here's the path a shopper takes from landing on your drop to completing an order (for exact screens and messaging, see [What your customers see](/guide/drop/what-customers-see)):

1. **Arrives** — Lands on your Fanfare-hosted page, a page on your own website with the Fanfare widget, or your Shopify product page. See [Brand, templates and designs](/concepts/design).
2. **Gets routed to one sequence** — Fanfare skips any sequence the shopper can't enter, then prefers an open sequence over a scheduled one, an ungated sequence they can enter right away over a gated one, and lower priority numbers first. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
3. **Passes the sequence's gates** — Signs in (or continues as a guest if the sequence has no audience and every open or scheduled distribution allows guests), enters an access code if required, and passes any audience check. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences) and [Customers and identity](/concepts/customers).
4. **Joins the waitlist (if early)** — Signed-in customers can join the sequence's waitlist whenever no distribution in that sequence is live and either a window is upcoming or none is scheduled yet. See [Waitlists](/concepts/waitlists).
5. **Participates in the open distribution** — Enters a [queue](/concepts/distributions/queue), [draw](/concepts/distributions/draw), [auction](/concepts/distributions/auction), [timed release](/concepts/distributions/timed-release), or books an [appointment](/concepts/distributions/appointment).
6. **Receives an admission** — Earns the right to buy within a timed payment window (appointments book a time slot instead and don't charge). See [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).
7. **Checks out** — Pays through Fanfare checkout, your store's checkout, or Shopify checkout. If a queue or draw on Fanfare checkout pre-authorized their card on entry, Fanfare captures the payment and creates the order automatically—no checkout step needed. If a payment window expires before they pay, they lose their spot and, in most cases, the unit returns to sale. See [Checkout options](/concepts/checkout-options) and [Admission and the payment window](/concepts/admission-and-payment).

## Status follows the clock

There's no separate "Launch" button in Fanfare. Each distribution's status updates automatically from its schedule—upcoming, live, ended, or deactivated early. A sequence rolls up its status from its distributions, and the experience rolls up from its sequences as **Draft** (no distributions yet), **Scheduled**, **Live**, **Ended early**, or **Ended**. Whenever at least one distribution is open, the entire experience shows **Live**. See [Experiences](/concepts/experiences).

## Key rules to keep in mind

- **Open beats scheduled, regardless of priority.** Priority follows the order in which you create sequences and can't be reordered in the admin. See [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).
- **Distributions inside one sequence can't overlap.** To run two release formats simultaneously, place them in separate sequences. See [Distributions](/concepts/distributions/overview).
- **Waitlists close the moment a sequence goes live.** Shoppers can only join while no distribution in the sequence is live and another window is still to come (or none has been added yet). See [Waitlists](/concepts/waitlists).
- **Stock management is permanent.** You choose whether Fanfare, Shopify, or nothing manages a product's stock when you create it, and that setting can't be changed later. See [Stock](/concepts/stock).
- **Plan changes never interrupt live drops.** Your plan governs what you can create and edit, not whether existing drops keep serving customers. See [Plans and what they unlock](/guide/start/plans).
- **Deactivating is permanent.** Once you deactivate a drop, sequence, or distribution, you can't undo it. See [What locks, and when](/concepts/what-locks-when).

## Example: VIP early access followed by a public draw

Suppose Northbound Supply Co. is launching the **Phantom Stitch Anorak Drop** with two sequences:

| Sequence | Priority | Who can enter | Distribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inner Circle Early Access** | `P1` | Members of the "Inner Circle" audience | **Inner Circle Queue** |
| **Public Draw** | `P2` | Everyone | **Public Draw** (opens after the queue closes) |

Each sequence has its own waitlist and its own dedicated share of stock.

- **An Inner Circle member visits while the queue is live:** Fanfare routes them to **Inner Circle Early Access** because it's open and highest priority. They sign in, pass the audience check, join the queue, and get a payment window once they reach the front.
- **A signed-in non-member visits at the same time:** Fanfare skips **Inner Circle Early Access** silently and routes them straight to **Public Draw**. Since the draw hasn't opened yet, they can join its waitlist and come back to enter once it opens.

For an hour-by-hour breakdown of how routing handles signed-in, signed-out, and guest visitors, see [Sequences and routing](/concepts/sequences).

## On Shopify

The underlying model—experiences, sequences, distributions, routing, and admissions—works the same on Shopify, with a few platform-specific differences:

- **No Appointments:** The Fanfare app in Shopify doesn't offer appointments.
- **Shopify catalog:** Products sync directly from your Shopify catalog. See [Products from Shopify](/guide/shopify/products).
- **Protected Shopify checkout:** Shoppers enter through the Fanfare widget on your product page and pay in Shopify checkout, where a checkout rule prevents anyone from buying a protected product without an admission. See [Checkout protection](/guide/shopify/checkout-protection).
