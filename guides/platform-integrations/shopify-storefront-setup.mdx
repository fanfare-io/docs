---
title: Shopify Storefront Setup
description: The two steps that connect the Fanfare app to your Shopify storefront, and how to adjust the product widget
---

Installing the Fanfare app is not the whole setup. Fanfare protects a product on your store only once two things are true:

1. **Checkout protection is on.** A checkout rule that runs on Shopify's servers refuses any checkout of a protected product that did not come through your experience.
2. **The product widget is on your theme.** A theme block shows the queue, draw, countdown or auction on the product page, in place of the buy buttons.

In the Fanfare app, **Settings → Storefront setup** shows the live status of both and links straight to the right place in Shopify admin. **Home** shows what is left until both are done.

## Before you start

- The Fanfare app is installed on your store.
- You can edit your store's checkout settings and its published theme.

## Step 1: Turn on checkout protection

Checkout protection is a Shopify checkout rule called **Fanfare checkout validation**. Without it, a shopper with a direct cart or checkout link can buy a protected product without waiting in your queue or winning your draw. Fanfare adds the rule and turns it on when you install the app, so on most stores this step is already done. Follow the steps below if **Storefront setup** shows it as **Not turned on** or **Turned off**.

1. In Shopify admin, open **Settings → Checkout** and scroll to **Checkout rules**.
2. If **Fanfare checkout validation** is not listed, select **Add rule** and choose it. If it is listed, open it.
3. Select **Turn on**.
4. Leave **Allow all customers to complete checkout** unticked, then select **Save**.

<Warning>
  With **Allow all customers to complete checkout** ticked, Shopify lets every order through whenever the rule cannot run. Fanfare treats that setting as protection being off.
</Warning>

Go back to the Fanfare app. **Storefront setup** rechecks when you return to it and shows **On**.

Fanfare never changes a rule you have edited. If you turn the rule off, it stays off until you turn it back on.

## Step 2: Add the product widget to your theme

The **Fanfare Product Widget** is a block for your product template.

1. In the Fanfare app, open **Settings → Storefront setup** and select **Add to product page**. The theme editor opens on your product template with the block ready to place.
2. Drag the block to where your buy buttons sit, usually just below the price.
3. Select **Save**.

To add it by hand instead: open **Online Store → Themes → Customize**, switch to a product template, select **Add block** in the product information section, and choose **Fanfare Product Widget** under **Apps**.

If your store uses more than one product template, add the block to each template that sells a protected product.

### What the widget changes on the page

While a product has a live experience, the widget hides:

- your **Add to cart** and **Buy it now** buttons
- express checkout buttons, such as Shop Pay and Apple Pay
- the variant and quantity pickers

A shopper who gets access picks their variant inside the widget and goes straight to checkout. The product title, price, images and description stay as they are.

A product with no live experience is untouched: the widget shows nothing and your theme's normal buy buttons appear.

## Adjust the widget

### Look and feel

Colours, fonts, button shape and wording are set in the Fanfare app under **Design Studio**, not in the theme editor, so every experience on your store matches. If you set no brand font, the widget uses your theme's font.

### Block settings

The block has two settings in the theme editor.

| Setting                   | What it does                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Product**               | The product whose experience the block shows. It defaults to the product whose page the block is on. Pick another to feature a product elsewhere.     |
| **Product Form Selector** | The CSS selector of your theme's buy-button area, which the widget hides during a live experience. The default, `.product-form`, fits Shopify's themes. |

### Preview before a drop

In the theme editor the block shows a preview panel with a **Hide product form** toggle. Use it to see exactly what shoppers will and will not see while an experience is live.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My buy buttons still show during a live experience">
    Your theme's buy-button area does not match the **Product Form Selector**. Open the block's settings in the theme editor and set the selector to the element that wraps your buy buttons. To hide several elements, separate their selectors with commas.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Storefront setup shows 'Couldn't check'">
    Fanfare could not read that setting from Shopify. Select **Check again**. If the product widget stays at **Couldn't check**, open the Fanfare app from Shopify admin and approve the updated permissions: reading your theme is how Fanfare knows the block is in place.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The widget is on the theme but shows nothing">
    That is expected for a product with no live experience. Create an experience for the product in the Fanfare app; the widget appears once the experience is live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shoppers reach checkout and are refused">
    Checkout protection refuses a protected product that was added to the cart outside the widget, for example through a direct cart link or a quick-add button elsewhere in your theme. Send shoppers to the product page, where the widget runs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
