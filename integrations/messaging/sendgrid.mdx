The SendGrid integration will provide an alternative email delivery service for transactional messages.

## Current Email Solution

Fanfare currently uses [Resend](/integrations/messaging/resend) for all transactional email delivery, providing:

- Reliable email delivery
- Delivery tracking and analytics
- Webhook events for status updates
- React-based email templates

## Why SendGrid?

SendGrid may be preferred for:

- Existing SendGrid infrastructure
- Specific compliance requirements
- Regional delivery preferences
- Enterprise support needs

## Planned Features

### Email Templates
