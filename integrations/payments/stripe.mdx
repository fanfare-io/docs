## Overview

Fanfare checkout takes consumer payments for draws, queues, auctions and timed releases through Stripe Connect. Payments settle to your organization's own Stripe account, and Fanfare takes its fee as an application fee on each payment.

There are two ways to set up your Stripe account:

| Setup path                               | How it works                                                                                                                                                                    | Where you manage the account                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Set up payments**                      | Fanfare creates and manages a Stripe account for your organization. You give Stripe your business and bank details in Stripe-hosted onboarding. You pay Stripe's fees on the account. | Account, payout and payment screens embedded in **Settings** > **Payments** |
| **Connect an existing Stripe account**   | You connect a Stripe account you already own by signing in to Stripe and authorizing Fanfare (OAuth).                                                                            | Your own Stripe Dashboard                                           |

## Prerequisites

- Fanfare checkout currently takes payments in US dollars, for organizations whose Stripe account is based in the United States.
- Stripe must have the business details it asks for before the account can take payments and pay out.

## How It Works

1. Your organization sets up or connects a Stripe account in **Settings** > **Payments**
2. A consumer pays through Fanfare checkout, and the payment is made on your Stripe account
3. Fanfare's fee is collected as a Stripe application fee on each payment
4. Stripe charges its processing fees to your Stripe account at your Stripe rates
5. Payouts go to your bank account on your Stripe payout schedule

```
┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│  Consumer   │────▶│   Fanfare   │────▶│ Your Stripe │
│  payment    │     │  checkout   │     │   account   │
└─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
                           ▲                    │
                           │   Application fee  │
                           └────────────────────┤
                                                ▼
                                         ┌─────────────┐
                                         │ Your bank   │
                                         │ (payouts)   │
                                         └─────────────┘
```

## Setup

### Step 1: Navigate to Payment Settings

1. Go to **Settings** > **Payments** in the Fanfare admin
2. You'll see the **Stripe account** card

### Step 2a: Set Up Payments

1. Click **Set up payments**
2. Fanfare creates a Stripe account for your organization and opens Stripe-hosted onboarding
3. Enter your business, identity and bank details
4. You're returned to Fanfare

If you leave onboarding before it's finished, click **Continue setup** to pick up where you left off.

### Step 2b: Connect an Existing Stripe Account

1. Click **Connect an existing Stripe account**
2. You're redirected to Stripe
3. Sign in to your Stripe account
4. Authorize Fanfare to access your account
5. You're redirected back to Fanfare

### Step 3: Verify Connection

After setup, the Stripe account card shows:

- Account name and account ID
- Email and country
- The account's status: Active, Action needed or Setup not finished

<Warning>
  **Account Verification**: If Stripe needs more information, payments or payouts can pause until you provide it.
</Warning>

## Connection Status

### Status Indicators

| Status                 | Description                                                    |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active**             | The account can take payments                                  |
| **Action needed**      | Stripe needs more information; payments or payouts can pause   |
| **Setup not finished** | Stripe hasn't received the account's details yet               |

### Providing What Stripe Asks For

| Setup path                             | Where to update details                                                     |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Set up payments**                    | The embedded account screens in **Settings** > **Payments**                 |
| **Connect an existing Stripe account** | Your Stripe Dashboard; click **Open Stripe Dashboard** on the Stripe account card |

Fanfare updates the status automatically when Stripe reports a change.

### Managing a Fanfare-Created Account

For an account created with **Set up payments**, **Settings** > **Payments** embeds Stripe's screens for:

- **Account and bank details**
- **Payouts**, including your payout schedule and Instant Payouts
- **Payments and disputes**

Refunds and captures are made by Fanfare, not from these screens.

## Webhooks

Fanfare receives updates from Stripe through webhooks, including:

| Event                              | Description                       |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `account.updated`                  | Connected account details changed |
| `account.application.deauthorized` | Account disconnected from Fanfare |
| `payment_intent.succeeded`         | A payment succeeded               |
| `payment_intent.payment_failed`    | A payment attempt failed          |
| `charge.dispute.created`           | A consumer disputed a payment     |

### Webhook Security

All Stripe webhooks are verified using their signatures:

```typescript
const event = await stripe.webhooks.constructEventAsync(body, signature, webhookSecret);
```

## Fanfare Checkout Fees

### How Fees Work

Fanfare takes a percentage of each paid order processed through Fanfare checkout:

- The rate depends on your Fanfare plan and the experience type: one rate for draws, queues and auctions, and a lower rate for timed releases
- Fanfare's fee is collected as a Stripe application fee on each paid order, and the rest settles to your Stripe account
- Stripe's processing fees are charged directly to your Stripe account at your Stripe rates

See [Pricing](/dashboard/billing/pricing) for each plan's rates, worked examples, and how refunds, disputes and payouts work.

### Viewing Your Rates

1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Payments**
2. Your current Fanfare checkout rates are displayed

<Info>
  Your rates come from your Fanfare plan. Enterprise rates are negotiated with Fanfare and can't be changed in settings.
</Info>

## Disconnecting

To disconnect your Stripe account:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Payments**
2. Click **Disconnect** on the Stripe account card
3. Confirm the disconnection

<Warning>
  **Warning**: Disconnecting stops Fanfare checkout from taking payments until you set up payments again.
</Warning>

### What Happens When You Disconnect

- No new payments can be taken through Fanfare checkout
- Historical transaction data remains in Fanfare
- Your organization stays tied to the same Stripe account; to use a different Stripe account, contact support

## API Reference

### Get Connection Status

Check the current Stripe Connect status.

```bash
GET /api/payment-providers/stripe/status
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "connected": true,
  "status": "ACTIVE",
  "accountId": "acct_1234567890",
  "displayName": "My Store",
  "chargesEnabled": true,
  "payoutsEnabled": true
}
```

### Start Fanfare-Created Account Onboarding

Create the organization's Fanfare-created Stripe account, or reuse it, and get a Stripe-hosted onboarding link.

```bash
POST /api/payment-providers/stripe/onboarding-link
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "url": "https://connect.stripe.com/setup/..."
}
```

### Connect an Existing Account

Get the OAuth URL to connect an existing Stripe account.

```bash
GET /api/payment-providers/stripe/auth/url
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "url": "https://connect.stripe.com/oauth/authorize?..."
}
```

### Disconnect Account

Remove the Stripe connection.

```bash
DELETE /api/payment-providers/stripe
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "success": true
}
```

## Payment Flows

### Auction Payments

When a consumer wins an auction:

1. Consumer receives a winning notification with a payment link
2. Consumer enters payment details
3. The payment is made on your Stripe account
4. Fanfare's application fee is collected
5. Order is marked as paid

### Queue/Draw Winner Payments

For experiences with pre-set pricing:

1. Winner receives checkout link
2. Payment is processed at checkout
3. Time limit enforced for checkout completion
4. Inventory released if payment not completed

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"Action needed" on the Stripe account card**

Stripe needs more information before the account can keep taking payments or paying out.

- **Set up payments**: provide the details in the embedded account screens in **Settings** > **Payments**
- **Connected existing account**: complete the pending steps in your Stripe Dashboard
- Wait for Stripe to review them

**OAuth callback error**

The connection flow was interrupted.

- Ensure you're logged into the correct Stripe account
- Check that browser cookies are enabled
- Try clearing cache and reconnecting

**"This organization is tied to a different Stripe account"**

Each organization uses one Stripe account. Connect the account your organization already uses, or contact support.

**Account disconnected unexpectedly**

The account owner may have revoked Fanfare's access from their Stripe Dashboard.

- Reconnect from **Settings** > **Payments**
- Contact Stripe support if issue persists

## Security

### Data Handling

- Fanfare never stores full card numbers
- Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe
- Only tokenized references are stored

### OAuth Security

- State parameter prevents CSRF attacks
- Tokens are exchanged server-side only
- Connected account IDs are validated

### Webhook Verification

- All webhooks require valid signatures
- Timestamps are validated to prevent replay attacks
- Events are processed idempotently
