## Current Payment Solution

Fanfare currently uses [Stripe Connect](/integrations/payments/stripe) for payment processing, providing:

- Credit/debit card payments
- Split payments via Connect
- Subscription billing
- Global coverage

## Why PayPal?

PayPal may be preferred for:

- Consumer payment preferences
- Buy now, pay later options
- International markets
- Alternative payment methods
